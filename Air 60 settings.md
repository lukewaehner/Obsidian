### `fn + q/w/e/r`
- Short press to swap to BT1/2/3 or 2.4ghz
- Long press for paring mode
- Be on 2.4ghz mode before plugging in dongle (i question this)

### `fn + uparrow / down arrow, left arrow, right arrow ; </>`
- up and down arrows control backlight brightness
- left arrow swipes through effects
- right arrow swipes through color switching
- < / > swipes through backlight speed

### `fn + m + arrows / carrots`
- sidelight brightness up / down
- sidelight effect switching left arrow
- sidelight color switching right arrow
- < / > are sidelight speed

### `fn + ]`
- Toggles sleep mode
