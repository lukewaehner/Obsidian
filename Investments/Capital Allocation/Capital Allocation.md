### Define Optimal:
- Quantify whether an investment is good / bad: Utility
- The total satisfaction or benefit from conusming a good or servic
	- Total satisfaction

### Utility and Risk Aversion
- How much satisfaction from this portfolio satisfaction?

- Investors are risk averse: they require return for risk
Prefers higher return -> higher utility for higher returns
Prefers low risk -> higher utility for lower returns

$$
U = E(r) - (\frac{1}{2})A\sigma^2
$$
- E(r) - expected return of the portfolio
- $\sigma^2$ - The variance of the portfolio
- And:

$$
A = \frac{E(r_p)-r_f}{0.5\sigma^2_p}
$$

Goal: Maximize $U's$ value.

- Risk Neutral (A = 0)
  Risk Averse (A > 0)
- Risk Loving (A < 0)
