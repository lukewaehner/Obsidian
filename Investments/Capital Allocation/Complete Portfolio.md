## Complete Portfolio

### Expected Return of the Complete Portfolio

$$
E(r_C) = y \times E(r_p) + (1-y)\times r_f
$$

where:

- $E(r_C) =$ Expected return of complete portfolio
- $E(r_p) =$ Expected Return of the risky portfolio
- $r_f =$ Return of the risk - free asset
- $y =$ % asset in the risky portfolio

### Standard Deviation of the Complete Portfolio

$$
\sigma_c = y \times \sigma_p + (1-y) \times 0 = y \times \sigma_p
$$

Where:

- $\sigma_c =$ Standard deviation of the complete portfolio
- $\sigma_p =$ Standard deviation of the risky portfolio

---

## Example

Given:

- $E(r_p) = 15\%$
- $\sigma_p = 0.22$
- $r_f = 7\%$

Assuming the percentage of money invested in risky-portfolio is **y**.

- Weight of risky portfolio is **y**
- What is the weight of risk-free asset? $1-y$
- What is the portfolio return? $E(r_c) = 7\% + 8\% \cdot y$
- The portfolio risk is just the weight of the risky asset $y$ times the standard deviation of the risky portfolio $\sigma_p$ or $\sigma_p \cdot y$
