Equity based assets
## Preferred:
- Fixed dividend
- Higher priority than common shareholders for dividends
## Common:
- Residual ownership in a corporation
	- receives a pro rated share of whatever is left over after all obligations are met, in the event of liquidation
- Interim Cash Flows through dividends, timing or amount is not guaranteed unless announced
- Capital Gain / Losses: value may rise or fall depending on company prospects and market-wide circumstances
- **Bid**: price people want to buy at
- **Ask**: price people want to sell at
- **Volume**: the amount of shares traded in a given day
- **Market Capitalization**: shares outstanding x share price 
	- ($10B+) = Large cap
	- ($2-10B) = Med cap
	- ($300M-$2B) = Small cap
- **Earning date**: the public date where a company announces financial performances for a specific period
- **Earnings Per Share**: 
- **P/E Ratio**: Price per share / Earnings per share
	- E.G. PE: 20 -> $20 invested for every $1 of the earning
	- Higher PE ratio means the company is overvalued, growth stocks
