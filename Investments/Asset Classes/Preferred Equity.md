Potential gains/losses:  
- Dividends are “expected” and/or “announced (promised).”  
- However, there is no legal requirement that the expected dividends be paid,  
as long as no common dividends are distributed.  
- By jurisdiction: announced may be seen as debt

and market-wide circumstances. (This is the same as common stock.)
