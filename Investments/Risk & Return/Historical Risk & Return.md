Goal is to see what financial market history can tell us about risk and return
- Two key observations
	- Substantial reward, on average, for bearing risk
	- Greater risks accompany greater returns
- These observations are important investment guidelines

## Note on The Historical Record
- **Past performance does not guarantee future results**
- Important lessons can still be learned in studying past performance
