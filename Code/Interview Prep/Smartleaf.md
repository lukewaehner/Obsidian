# **About Us**

Smartleaf improves money management for everyone.

With our help, consistent, high-quality financial advice can be offered to people
of all wealth levels. End investors can customize their portfolios
according to environmental and social values and enjoy the same sophisticated
analytics, tax management and customization that were previously available
only to multi-millionaires.
Financial advisors can scale their business without sacrificing quality.

We work with wealth management firms of all types, from multi-billion-dollar
national banks to startup “robo advisors”.

## **What You’ll Gain**

You will work on production code.  Working on a small team, everything you do has a direct impact on the team, the product and our clients.  We expect our coops to be independent and take ownership of substantial projects.

You’ll do hands-on development work on a complex financial application.

You’ll gain experience working in a cross-functional team. We handle front- and back- end development, integrations, API implementations, database work and customer support.

You will engage in all aspects of the development lifecycle: project planning, technical design, implementation, quality assurance, release management and support.  You will gain experience in the agile development process.

In addition, you will gain experience working with the Product Management, QA and Financial Engineering teams.  We are a collaborative workplace and you can expect a lot of interaction with your coworkers.

**Responsibilities**

- Implementing features and bugfixes (UI, back end and API)
