## Introduction
---
- Many constructs look the same and have similar semantics to Java
- C is a front-end for assembly
---
- Created by Dennis Ritchie and Ken Thompson during their work on Unix at Bell Labs
- C was intended to make programming Unix easier
- The early versions were in Assembly
- C succeeds B
	- B is based on a language called BCPL
- High-Level compared to Assembly, low level conceptually
- Types kind of strong
- You can manage memory
- You can even inline assembly
