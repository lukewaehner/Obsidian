
---

## Operating Activities

Activities related to daily routine business transactions that generate revenues and incur expenses.

---

## Two Methods

### Direct Method

Identify specific cash inflows and outflows.

### Indirect Method (Preferred)

Start with net income, then adjust for non-cash items.

**Adjustments:**

- Add back: Depreciation expense
- Add/Subtract: Gain/Loss on disposal of PP&E
- Adjust for changes in: A/R, Inventory, A/P, etc.

---

## Why Indirect is Preferred

- Easier and less costly to prepare
- Focuses on differences between net income and net cash flow

---
