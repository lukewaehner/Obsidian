
---

## Financing Activities

Activities related to obtaining cash from debt and equity, and returning cash to investors.

---

## Cash Inflows

- Issuance of bonds
- Issuance of stock
- Borrowing (notes payable)

## Cash Outflows

- Redemption of bonds
- Repurchase of stock (treasury stock)
- Payment of dividends
- Repayment of loans

---

## Example

1. Issued $1,800 bonds
2. Redeemed $400 bonds for cash
3. Issued common stock for $500
4. Paid dividends of $300

```
Cash flows from financing activities:
  Issuance of bonds                   1,800
  Redemption of bonds                  (400)
  Issuance of common stock              500
  Dividends paid                       (300)
Net cash inflow from financing        1,600
```
