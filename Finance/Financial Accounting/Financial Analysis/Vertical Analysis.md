
---

## Vertical Analysis

Also known as **common-size analysis**.

Expresses each item as a percentage of a base amount.

---

## Base Amounts

- **Balance Sheet**: Total Assets
- **Income Statement**: Net Sales

---

## Purpose

Show relative size of each item on financial statements.

Compare companies of different sizes.

---

## Example

If Cash = $50,000 and Total Assets = $500,000:

Cash as % of Total Assets = $50,000 / $500,000 = 10%
