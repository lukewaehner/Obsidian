## Accounting for Plant Assets

### Types of Plant Assets

- Land (not depreciated)
- Land Improvements (fences, parking lots, driveways)
- Buildings
- Equipment and Machinery

### Recording at Cost (Historical Cost Principle)

Cost includes all expenditures necessary to acquire and make ready for use:

- Purchase price
- Sales taxes
- Freight
- Installation
- Upgrades, additions, improvements

### Cost Measurement

- Cash paid in cash transactions, or
- Cash equivalent price when non-cash assets are used

---

## Note

Freight costs to acquire inventory are added to inventory cost, but shipping costs to customers are a selling expense.
