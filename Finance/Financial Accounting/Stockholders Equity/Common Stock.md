## Common Stock

Basic ownership shares with voting rights.

### Stock Terminology

- **Authorized Stock**: Maximum shares corporation can sell (per charter)
- **Issued Stock**: Shares actually sold to investors
- **Outstanding Stock**: Shares held by investors (issued - treasury)

### Par Value

A value assigned to each share in the charter.

---

## Issuing Common Stock at Par

Sell 1,000 shares of $1 par value stock for $5/share:

```
Dr. Cash                              5,000
    Cr. Common Stock                          1,000
    Cr. Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par      4,000
```

---

## No-Par Stock with Stated Value

Sell 5,000 shares of $5 stated value no-par stock at $8/share:

```
Dr. Cash                                     40,000
    Cr. Common Stock                                 25,000
    Cr. Paid-in Capital in Excess of Stated Value   15,000
```

---

## No-Par Stock without Stated Value

Sell 5,000 shares at $8/share:

```
Dr. Cash               40,000
    Cr. Common Stock           40,000
```
