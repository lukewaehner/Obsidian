## Dividends

Distribution of cash or stock to stockholders on a pro rata (proportional) basis.

### Three Important Dates

1. **Declaration Date**: Board announces dividend
2. **Record Date**: Determines eligible stockholders
3. **Payment Date**: Dividend is paid

---

## Cash Dividends

### On Declaration Date

```
Dr. Cash Dividends            50,000
    Cr. Dividends Payable             50,000
```

### On Record Date

No journal entry needed.

### On Payment Date

```
Dr. Dividends Payable         50,000
    Cr. Cash                          50,000
```

---

## Stock Dividends

Pro rata distribution of corporation's own stock.

- Does not decrease total equity or total assets
- Retained Earnings decreases, Paid-in Capital increases

### Small Stock Dividend (< 25%)

Use market price to value.

**Declare 10% stock dividend on 10,000 shares (par $1, market $5):**

New shares: 10,000 × 10% = 1,000

```
Dr. Stock Dividends                    5,000
    Cr. Common Stock Dividends Distributable   1,000
    Cr. Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par       4,000
```

**On Payment Date:**

```
Dr. Common Stock Dividends Distributable  1,000
    Cr. Common Stock                              1,000
```
