## Equity

Ownership (residual) claim on a corporation's total assets.

$$
\text{Equity} = \text{Common Stock} + \text{Retained Earnings}
$$

$$
\text{Retained Earnings} = \text{Revenues} - \text{Expenses} - \text{Dividends}
$$

---

## Components

| Account | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Common Stock | Money paid in by stockholders |
| Retained Earnings | Cumulative profits after paying out dividends |
| Dividends | Money distributed to stockholders (not an expense) |
| Revenues | Economic benefits from sales of goods or services |
| Expenses | Economic costs of assets consumed or services used |

---

## Expanded Accounting Equation

$$
\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Common Stock} + \text{Beg. R/E} + \text{Revenues} - \text{Expenses} - \text{Dividends}
$$
