## Unearned Revenue

Cash received before the service is performed. Recorded as a liability until earned.

---

## Example

On 10/12/2020, company receives $10,000 cash for consulting services to be provided in November.

**On 10/12/2020:**

```
Dr. Cash                 10,000
    Cr. Unearned Revenue         10,000
```

**In November (when service is provided):**

```
Dr. Unearned Revenue     10,000
    Cr. Service Revenue          10,000
```

---

## Another Example

On September 4, received $3,240 cash for sponsoring a game each month for 9 months.

**On 9/4:**

```
Dr. Cash                  3,240
    Cr. Unearned Service Revenue  3,240
```

**On 12/31 (4 months earned: Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec):**

$3,240 / 9 × 4 = $1,440

```
Dr. Unearned Service Revenue  1,440
    Cr. Service Revenue           1,440
```

Ending balance in Unearned Service Revenue: $3,240 - $1,440 = $1,800
