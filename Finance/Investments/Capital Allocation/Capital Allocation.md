## Capital Allocation

Balance between 1 risk-free and 1 risky asset.

Examples:

- 30% to US T-bills, 70% to SPY
- 50% to 3-month government bond, 50% to Apple

---

## Investment Decisions

1. **Capital Allocation** - Balance between risk-free and risky assets
2. **Asset Allocation** - Balance between risky portfolios / broad risky asset classes
3. **Security Selection** - Alpha seeking valuation

---

## Finding Optimal Capital Allocation

1. Define Optimal
2. Find the opportunity set: What are the possible portfolios available to investors
3. When (1) meets (2)
