## Purpose
- Facilitate low-cost investment
- Provide liquidity
- Minimize time to trade
- Promote price continuity
- Set and update security prices
- Reduce information costs

## Examples
- Stock exchanges (NYSE, NASDAQ)
- Over-the-counter (OTC) markets

## Market-Depth
Ability to absorb relatively large market orders without significantly impacting the price of the security
