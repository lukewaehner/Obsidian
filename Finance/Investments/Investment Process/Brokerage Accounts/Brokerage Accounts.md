## Definition
Accounts that allow investors to buy and sell securities through brokers.

## Types
- Full-service broker: personalized advice, higher fees
- Discount broker: lower fees, execution-only
- Online brokerage accounts

## Functions
- Hold cash and securities
- Enable margin trading
- Provide access to order types (market, limit, stop)
