## Expected and Unexpected Returns

The return on any stock is composed of two parts:

- The normal, or **expected** part of the return is the return that investors predict or expect
- The uncertain, or **risky** part of the return comes from *unexpected information* revealed during the year
- Total Return = Expected Return + Unexpected Return

In a given year, the unexpected return $(U)$ will be positive or negative, but its average value should be zero.

$$
U = R - E(R)
$$

## Announcements and News

Firm announcements are events that impact profits:

- Earnings
- Product development
- Personnel changes

The impact of an announcement depends on how much is new.

- When the situation is not as bad as previously thought, bad is good
- When the situation is not as good as previously thought, good is bad

In an efficient market, the market's expectations of events should already be priced in.

$$
\text{Announcement} = \text{Expected News} + \text{Surprise News}
$$

Only the surprise news should impact the stock price.
