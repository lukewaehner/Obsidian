Discount rate that equates today's bond price with the present value of the future cash flows of the bond
$$
P = \sum_{t=1}^{T} \frac{C}{(1 + y)^t} + \frac{F}{(1 + y)^T}
$$

Where:
- $P$ = current bond price
- $C$ = coupon payment per period
- $F$ = Face (par) value
- $y$ = YTM
- $T$ = number of periods to maturity

## What decides YTM:
- What should YTM be?

- How risk is the bonds?
	- Interest Rate Risk
	- Default Risk

- Default Risk:
	- Firm specific
	- Bond specific
		- Some bonds may default, some won't.
