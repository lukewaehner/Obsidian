
Mutual funds are categorized by their investment objectives, time horizons, and asset holdings.

---

## Overview

What kinds of mutual fund products are available in the market? Investment objective is the major determinant of fund type.

---

## Short-Term Funds

### Money Market Funds
- [[Money Market Mutual Funds (MMMFs)]] - Short-term, cash-like investments

---

## Long-Term Funds

Traditionally classified as stock funds, bond funds, or balanced funds, based on investment objective.

### Equity Funds
*See notes in [[Stock Funds]] folder*

**By Investment Objective:**
- [[Capital Appreciation Funds]]
- [[Growth Funds]]
- [[Growth and Income Funds]]
- [[Equity Income Funds]]

**By Company Size:**
- [[Small-Cap Funds]]
- [[Mid-Cap Funds]]
- [[Large-Cap Funds]]

**By Geography:**
- [[Global Funds]]
- [[International Funds]]
- [[Regional Funds]]
- [[Country Funds]]
- [[Emerging Markets Funds]]

**By Sector:**
- [[Sector Funds]] - Industry-specific investments

**Other Equity Types:**
- [[Index Funds]]
- [[Social Conscience Funds]] - Green/ESG funds
- [[Sin Funds]] - Tobacco, liquor, gaming
- [[Tax-Managed Funds]]

### Fixed Income Funds
*See notes in [[Bond Funds]] folder*

**Distinguished by:**
- Maturity range
- Taxability
- Bond type
- Issuing country
- Credit quality

**Types Include:**
- [[Short-Term Bond Funds]]
- [[Intermediate-Term Bond Funds]]
- [[Single-State Municipal Funds]]
- [[High-Yield Bond Funds]]
- [[Mortgage Bond Funds]]
- [[General Bond Funds]]
- [[World Bond Funds]]
- [[Insured Bond Funds]]

### Blended/Hybrid Funds
- [[Balanced Funds]] - Mix of stocks and bonds
- [[Asset Allocation Funds]] - Dynamic allocation
- [[Income Funds]] - Focus on income generation

### Target Date Funds
- [[Target Date Funds]] - Lifecycle funds that adjust allocation over time

---

## Fund Selection Criteria

When choosing a fund, consider:
- Investment objective alignment
- Risk tolerance
- Time horizon
- Geographic exposure
- Sector concentration
- Tax implications
