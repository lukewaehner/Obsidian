## Key Comparisons

### Open-End vs Closed-End
| Feature | Open-End | Closed-End |
|---------|----------|------------|
| Share Issuance | Issues/redeems continuously | Fixed number of shares |
| Trading | Through fund at NAV | On exchange |
| Pricing | Price = NAV | Price ≠ NAV |
