## Share Structure

### [[Share Classes]]
Different fee structures for different investor types

**Retail Investors:**
- **Class A** - Front-end load, low annual fees (long-term)
- **Class C** - No front-end load, high annual fees (short-term)

**Institutional Investors:**
- **Class I (or Y)** - No loads, no 12b-1, high minimums

#### Class Comparison
| Class | Front Load | Back Load | 12b-1 | Minimum | Best For |
|-------|-----------|-----------|-------|---------|----------|
| A | 4.5% | 0% | 0.25% | $1,000 | Long-term |
| C | 0% | 1% (1yr) | 1% | $1,000 | Short-term/Uncertain |
| I/Y | 0% | 0% | 0% | $5M+ | Institutions |

## Fund Information Access

### What Investors Need to Know
> "What should we know about the mutual fund we are interested in:  
> who manages, fees, returns over time, risk, cost, NAV, etc."

Key information to research:
- Fund manager and tenure
- [[Fees & Costs|Fee structure]]
- Historical performance
- Risk metrics
- Current [[Net Asset Value (NAV)|NAV]]
- Investment strategy
- Holdings

### Where to Find Information
1. **Prospectus** - Comprehensive legal document
2. **Fact sheets** - Quick summaries
3. **Annual/Semi-annual reports** - Performance updates
4. **Fund company website** - Updated data
5. **Third-party research** - Morningstar, etc.

## Regulatory Requirements

### Mandatory Disclosures
Funds must provide by law:
1. **[[Fund Prospectus|Prospectus]]** to any potential investor
2. **Periodic reports** to current shareholders
3. **Expense tables** in standardized format
4. **Performance data** with required disclaimers

### Investor Protections
- Regulated by SEC
- Required transparency
- Fiduciary duties
- Redemption rights (open-end funds)

## Fund Creation and Management

### Who Creates Funds
- [[Investment Advisory Firms]] (e.g., Fidelity, Vanguard)
- Brokerage firms with investment advisory operations (e.g., Merrill Lynch)

### Management Structure
- [[Investment Advisory Firms]] earn fees for managing funds
- One company can manage many different funds
- Professional [[Fund Managers]] make investment decisions
