

**Definition:** The legal document that mutual funds are required by law to provide to any investor who wishes to purchase shares.

## Legal Requirement

> "They are required by law to supply a prospectus to any investor who wishes to purchase shares."

### Regulatory Basis
- Required by Securities Act of 1933
- Enforced by SEC (Securities and Exchange Commission)
- Must be provided **before or at time of purchase**
- Failure to provide = securities law violation

## Purpose

### Primary Function
- Full disclosure of fund details
- Enable informed investment decisions
- Provide legally binding information
- Protect investors through transparency

### Key Sections

#### Fee Information

- Fund objectives
- Investment strategies
- Principal risks
- Performance history

- How to buy/redeem shares
- Minimum investments

#### Other Required Disclosures
- Tax information
- Financial highlights
- How advisors are compensated

## Types of Prospectuses

### Summary Prospectus
- Shorter version (3-5 pages)
- Key information only
- Most common format

### Statutory Prospectus
- Full legal document (50-100+ pages)
- Complete disclosure
- Available upon request

## How to Access

### Delivery Methods
1. Physical mail
2. Electronic (email/website)
3. Online (fund website)
4. Through broker platform

### Required Timing
- Before or at time of investment
- Annual updates sent to shareholders

## What to Focus On

### Priority Sections
1. **Fee table** - Compare costs
2. **Investment objective** - What fund aims to achieve
3. **Principal risks** - What can go wrong
4. **Performance data** - Historical returns
5. **Manager information** - Who runs it

### Red Flags
- Expense ratio > 1.5% (equity)
- Expense ratio > 1.0% (bonds)
- Frequent manager changes
- Complex, hard-to-understand strategies
- Poor performance vs. peers
