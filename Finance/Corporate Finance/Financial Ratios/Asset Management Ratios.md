## Asset Management Ratios (Turnover Ratios)

Measure efficiency of asset utilization.

---

## Inventory Turnover

$$
\text{Inventory Turnover} = \frac{\text{COGS}}{\text{Inventory}}
$$

How many times inventory is sold and replaced per period.

$$
\text{Days' Sales in Inventory} = \frac{365}{\text{Inventory Turnover}}
$$

---

## Receivables Turnover

$$
\text{Receivables Turnover} = \frac{\text{Sales}}{\text{Accounts Receivable}}
$$

How effectively company collects receivables.

$$
\text{Days' Sales in Receivables} = \frac{365}{\text{Receivables Turnover}}
$$

---

## Total Asset Turnover

$$
\text{Total Asset Turnover} = \frac{\text{Sales}}{\text{Total Assets}}
$$

Efficiency in using assets to generate sales.
