
---

## Leverage Ratios (Long-term Solvency)

Measure ability to survive long-term.

---

## Total Debt Ratio

$$
\text{Total Debt Ratio} = \frac{\text{Total Assets} - \text{Total Equity}}{\text{Total Assets}}
$$

Proportion of assets financed through debt.

---

## Debt-to-Equity Ratio

$$
\text{Debt-to-Equity} = \frac{\text{Total Debt}}{\text{Total Equity}}
$$

Higher ratio = more reliance on debt = more risk.

---

## Equity Multiplier

$$
\text{Equity Multiplier} = \frac{\text{Total Assets}}{\text{Total Equity}}
$$

High multiplier indicates greater reliance on debt.

---

## Times Interest Earned (TIE)

$$
\text{TIE} = \frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Interest}}
$$

Ability to meet interest payments. Higher is better.

---

## Cash Coverage Ratio

$$
\text{Cash Coverage} = \frac{\text{EBIT} + \text{Depreciation}}{\text{Interest}}
$$

Includes depreciation to measure cash available for interest.
