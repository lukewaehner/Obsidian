## Incremental Free Cash Flow Calculation

Convert incremental earnings to incremental free cash flows.

---

## Formula

$$
\text{FCF} = \text{EBIT}(1-t) + \text{Depreciation} - \text{CapEx} - \Delta\text{NWC}
$$

---

## Why Adjust Earnings?

Earnings include non-cash items (depreciation) and exclude capital investments and working capital changes.

---

## Example

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue | $79,000 |
| Costs | $39,500 |
| Depreciation | $13,000 |
| Tax Rate | 34% |
| CapEx | $53,000 |
| ΔCurrent Assets | $18,000 |
| ΔCurrent Liabilities | $14,000 |

**EBIT** = $79,000 - $39,500 - $13,000 = $26,500

**Operating Cash Flow:**

$OCF = \$26,500 \times (1-0.34) + \$13,000 = \$30,490$

**ΔNWC:**

$\Delta NWC = \$18,000 - \$14,000 = \$4,000$

**Free Cash Flow:**

$FCF = \$30,490 - \$53,000 - \$4,000 = -\$26,510$
