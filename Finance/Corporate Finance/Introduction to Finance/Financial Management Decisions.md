## Financial Management Decisions

### Three Key Questions

1. **Capital Budgeting**: What long-term investments should you take on?
   - Assets, lines of business, buildings, machinery, equipment

2. **Capital Structure**: Where will you get long-term financing?
   - Liabilities: Bonds, bank loans
   - Equity: Common stock, preferred stock, retained earnings

3. **Working Capital Management**: How will you manage everyday financial activities?
   - Collecting from customers
   - Paying suppliers
