## EAR vs APR

### Annual Percentage Rate (APR)

- Quoted rate that does not account for compounding
- APR = r × m (where r is periodic rate, m is periods per year)

### Effective Annual Rate (EAR)

- Total interest earned at end of one year with compounding
- The "true" annual rate

---

## Conversion Formula

$$
EAR = \left(1 + \frac{APR}{m}\right)^m - 1
$$

Where m = number of compounding periods per year.

---

## Example

1-year auto loan with 10% APR, monthly compounding:

$$
EAR = \left(1 + \frac{0.10}{12}\right)^{12} - 1 = 10.47\%
$$

---

## Key Insight

EAR is always greater than or equal to APR when compounding more than once per year.
