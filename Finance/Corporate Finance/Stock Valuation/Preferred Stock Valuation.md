## Preferred Stock Valuation

Preferred stock is a hybrid security with features of both stocks and bonds.

---

## Like Common Stock

- No maturity
- Failure to pay dividends does not lead to bankruptcy

## Like Bonds

- No voting rights
- Fixed dividends (cumulative or non-cumulative)

---

## Valuation

Preferred stock pays same dividend forever = perpetuity.

$$
P_0 = \frac{Div}{r}
$$

---

## Example

Samsung preferred stock: Constant annual dividend of $1.80, required return 10%

$$
P_0 = \frac{\$1.80}{0.10} = \$18.00
$$
