## Income Statement

Summarizes a firm's revenues and expenses over a given period of time.

---

## Structure

```
Net Sales
- COGS (raw material, direct labor, manufacturing overhead)
- SG&A (company president salary, etc.)
= EBITDA (Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation & amortization)
- Depreciation
= EBIT (Earnings before interest and taxes)
- Interest Expenses
= EBT (Earnings before taxes)
- Taxes
= Net Income (NI)
```

---

## Non-Cash Items

Primary reason accounting income differs from cash flow is non-cash items.

**Depreciation & Amortization** is most important:
- Matches expense of purchasing asset with benefits produced
- Reduces net income but no cash leaves the firm

---

## Example

Food Truck Business buys truck for $30,000, depreciates over 10 years straight-line:

Annual depreciation = $30,000 / 10 = $3,000

This year: $3,000 less net income, but you spent $30,000 in cash (in year 0).
