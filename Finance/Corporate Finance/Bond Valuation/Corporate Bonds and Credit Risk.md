## Corporate Bonds and Credit Risk

Corporate bonds have default risk, so expected return is not equal to YTM.

---

## Default Risk (Credit Risk)

The possibility that the issuer cannot make timely principal and interest payments.

Because of this risk, corporate bonds trade at lower prices (higher yields) than comparable Treasury securities.

---

---

## Yield Spread

Corporate bond yield = Treasury yield + Credit spread

The credit spread compensates for default risk.

---

## Example

5-year A-rated corporate bond:

YTM = 5-year Treasury YTM + A-rated spread

If Treasury YTM = 3.8% and A-rated spread = 0.9%:

Corporate YTM = 3.8% + 0.9% = **4.7%**
