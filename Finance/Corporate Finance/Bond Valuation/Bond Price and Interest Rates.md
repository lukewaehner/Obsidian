
---

## Bond Price and Interest Rates

**Bond prices and interest rates move inversely.**

---

## Price Sensitivity

Longer maturity bonds are more sensitive to interest rate changes.

---

## Example

Two bonds: 1-year (A) and 30-year (B), both $1,000 face, 3% semi-annual coupon.

**At 12% market rate:**
- Bond A: $917.50
- Bond B: $272.74

**At 15% market rate:**
- Bond A: $892.26 (-2.75%)
- Bond B: $210.43 (-22.84%)

The 30-year bond price changed much more dramatically.

---

## Premium vs Discount

- **At Premium**: Price > Face Value (Coupon Rate > Market Rate)
- **At Par**: Price = Face Value (Coupon Rate = Market Rate)
- **At Discount**: Price < Face Value (Coupon Rate < Market Rate)
