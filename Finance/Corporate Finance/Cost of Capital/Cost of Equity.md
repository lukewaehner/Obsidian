## Cost of Equity

The return shareholders require to invest in the firm.

---

## Two Methods

### 1. CAPM

$$
r_E = r_f + \beta(E[R_{Mkt}] - r_f)
$$

### 2. Constant Dividend Growth Model

$$
r_E = \frac{D_1}{P_0} + g
$$

---

## Best Practice

Average the two estimates, as both have limitations.

---

## Example

Runtan Inc.:
- Annual dividend: $0.45
- Growth rate: 7%
- Stock price: $37.10
- Beta: 0.5
- Risk-free rate: 2%
- Market return: 8%

**DDM:**

$$
r_E = \frac{\$0.45 \times 1.07}{\$37.10} + 0.07 = 8.30\%
$$

**CAPM:**

$$
r_E = 2\% + 0.5 \times (8\% - 2\%) = 5.0\%
$$

**Average:**

$$
r_E = \frac{8.30\% + 5.0\%}{2} = 6.65\%
$$
