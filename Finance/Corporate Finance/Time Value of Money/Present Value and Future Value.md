## Present Value and Future Value

### Finding Future Value

$$
FV = PV \times (1 + r)^n
$$

**Example:** Deposit $4,000 at 2% for 5 years

$FV = 4000 \times (1.02)^5 = \$4,416.32$

---

### Finding Present Value

$$
PV = \frac{FV}{(1 + r)^n}
$$

**Example:** Receive $40,000 in 21 years at 6%

$PV = \frac{40000}{(1.06)^{21}} = \$11,789.37$

---

### Finding Interest Rate

$$
r = \left(\frac{FV}{PV}\right)^{1/n} - 1
$$

**Example:** Have $1,000, want $10,000 in 5 years

$r = (10000/1000)^{1/5} - 1 = 58.49\%$

---

### Finding Number of Periods

$$
n = \frac{\ln(FV/PV)}{\ln(1 + r)}
$$

**Example:** Have $4,000, want $10,000 at 3%

$n = \frac{\ln(10000/4000)}{\ln(1.03)} = 31 \text{ years}$
