## TVM Fundamentals

### Notation

- **PV** - Present value (earlier, at "time 0")
- **FV** - Future value (later, at "time n")
- **r** - Interest rate per period
- **n** - Number of compounding periods

Interest rate and number of periods must have the same frequency.

---

## Three Rules

1. **Only values at the same point in time can be compared or combined**

2. **To calculate future value, compound:**

$$
FV_n = PV \times (1 + r)^n
$$

3. **To calculate present value, discount:**

$$
PV = \frac{FV_n}{(1 + r)^n}
$$

---

## Sign Convention

- Cash flows **in** are positive
- Cash flows **out** are negative
