## Measuring Returns

### Realized Return

$$
R_{t+1} = \frac{Div_{t+1} + P_{t+1} - P_t}{P_t} = \text{Dividend Yield} + \text{Capital Gains Yield}
$$

---

### Expected Return

With probability distribution:

$$
E(R) = \sum_{i=1}^{N} Pr_i \times R_i
$$

---

### Average Returns

**Arithmetic Mean:**

$$
\bar{R} = \frac{1}{T}(R_1 + R_2 + ... + R_T)
$$

Average return in a typical year.

**Geometric Mean:**

$$
\bar{R} = [(1+R_1)(1+R_2)...(1+R_T)]^{1/T} - 1
$$

Average compound return per year.

---

### Example

Stock: $100 → $50 → $100

Year 1: -50%, Year 2: +100%

- Arithmetic mean: (-50% + 100%)/2 = **25%**
- Geometric mean: [(0.5)(2)]^0.5 - 1 = **0%**

Geometric mean better reflects actual wealth change.
