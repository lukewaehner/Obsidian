## Measuring Risk

### Variance

$$
Var(R) = \sum_{i=1}^{N} Pr_i \times (R_i - E(R))^2
$$

---

### Standard Deviation

$$
\sigma(R) = \sqrt{Var(R)}
$$

More intuitive than variance (same units as returns).

---

### Example

| State | Probability | Return |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0.1 | -16.7% |
| 2 | 0.2 | -6.7% |
| 3 | 0.4 | 0% |
| 4 | 0.2 | 16.7% |
| 5 | 0.1 | 50% |

Expected Return = 5.33%

Variance = 0.0314

Standard Deviation = 17.72%
