# Intro to Strategy

**Definition**: Strategy is the set of goal-directed actions a firm takes to gain and sustain superior performance relative to its competitors — a [[What is Strategy? and Five Forces|sustainable competitive advantage]].

## What Is Strategy?

Strategy is about creating a **unique and valuable position** through three interlocking choices:

- Creating a unique and valuable position
- Making **trade-offs** — deciding what *not* to do
- Creating a **fit** among a company's activities so they reinforce one another

Ultimately, strategy is about **goal-directed actions for maintaining sustained competitive advantage** — understanding what causes superior versus subpar performance.

> Strategy is *not* just operational effectiveness. Doing the same activities better than rivals is valuable, but the "meta" gets copied — operational gains erode as competitors imitate them.

### Strategic Positioning

Strategic positioning attempts to achieve sustainable competitive advantage by:

- Performing **different activities** than rivals, or
- Performing **similar activities in different ways**

## Competitive Advantage vs. Sustainable Competitive Advantage

| | Competitive Advantage | Sustainable Competitive Advantage (SCA) |
|---|---|---|
| **Definition** | Superior performance relative to competitors | Superior performance *sustained over time* |
| **Time horizon** | Point in time | 2y · 5y · 10y · >10y |
| **Key condition** | Outperform rivals or the industry average | Rivals **cannot duplicate** the value, or it is **too costly** to justify duplicating |

**Competitive Advantage**
- Superior performance relative to competitors

**Sustainable Competitive Advantage**
- Superior performance sustained over a period of time (2y, 5y, 10y, >10y)
- Occurs when a firm creates value that competitors are unable to duplicate, or that is too costly to justify duplicating

## How Do We Make Money?

Strategy operates at two levels, each answering a different question:

| Level | Question | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Corporate strategy** | What businesses are attractive? | Which markets/industries to compete in |
| **Business strategy** | How should we effectively compete? | How to win within a chosen market |
