Balance sheet:
You buy 1,000 PFE shares at $39 per share.
You put up **$30,000** to borrow the rest

What is the amount borrowed?
$$
\$39 * 1,000 = \$39,000 - \$30,000 = $9,000
$$

What is your Liability?
	The amount borrowed

What percent do you have in the margin?
$$
\frac{30,000}{39,000} = 77\%
$$
