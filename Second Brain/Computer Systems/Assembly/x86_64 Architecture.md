## Topic:
---
- 64-bit variant of the **x86 architecture**
- Roots: Intel 4004 → 8008 (8-bit) → 8086 (16-bit) → 80286 → 80386 (32-bit)
- AMD introduced first 64-bit x86 processor: **Athlon64**
- All x86 CPUs are backward-compatible with 8086

**Bit width meaning**
- 8-bit: 8 data lines
- 16-bit: 16 data lines (8086 actually had 20-bit addressing)
- 32-bit: 80386
- 64-bit: modern x86_64
