## Portfolio Risk and Return

### Portfolio Return

$$
R_p = \sum_{i=1}^{N} w_i R_i = w_1 R_1 + w_2 R_2 + ... + w_N R_N
$$

Where $w_i$ = weight of asset i (weights sum to 1).

---

### Portfolio Variance (Two Assets)

$$
Var(R_p) = w_1^2 \sigma_1^2 + w_2^2 \sigma_2^2 + 2w_1 w_2 Cov(R_1, R_2)
$$

---

### Covariance

Measures how returns move together.

$$
Cov(R_A, R_B) = \sum_{T=1}^{N} Pr_T \times (R_{AT} - \bar{R}_A) \times (R_{BT} - \bar{R}_B)
$$

---

### Correlation

$$
Corr(R_A, R_B) = \rho_{AB} = \frac{Cov(R_A, R_B)}{\sigma_A \sigma_B}
$$

Correlation ranges from -1 to +1.

- ρ = +1: Perfect positive correlation
- ρ = 0: Uncorrelated
- ρ = -1: Perfect negative correlation

---

### Key Insight

Imperfect correlation (ρ < 1) enables diversification to reduce portfolio risk.
