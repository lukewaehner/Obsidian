## Systematic vs Unsystematic Risk

### Total Risk

$$
\text{Total Risk} = \text{Systematic Risk} + \text{Unsystematic Risk}
$$

---

### Systematic Risk

Also called: Market risk, Non-diversifiable risk

Risk affecting the entire economy:
- GDP changes
- Interest rates
- Inflation
- War
- Pandemic

**Cannot be eliminated through diversification.**

---

### Unsystematic Risk

Also called: Firm-specific risk, Diversifiable risk, Idiosyncratic risk

Risk affecting individual companies:
- Product announcements
- Management changes
- Labor strikes

**Can be eliminated through diversification.**

---

### Principle of Diversification

As you add more stocks, unsystematic risk approaches zero.

A well-diversified portfolio has only systematic risk.

---

### Systematic Risk Principle

Only **systematic** risk affects expected return and risk premium.

Since unsystematic risk can be eliminated at no cost through diversification, investors are not compensated for bearing it.
