
---

## Project Cost of Capital

The discount rate depends on project risk, not firm risk.

---

## Key Principle

Cost of capital depends on the **use** of funds, not the **source** of funds.

---

## When to Use WACC

Only for projects with **same risk as firm average**.

---

## Multi-Division Firms

Different divisions may have different risks.

- Should NOT use single company-wide WACC
- Should use division-specific or project-specific WACC

---

## Danger of Single WACC

Using one WACC for all projects will:
- Accept too many risky projects (their hurdle rate is too low)
- Reject too many safe projects (their hurdle rate is too high)

---

## Example

Firm with WACC = 8.3%, 30% equity, 70% debt:

| Project | Risk | Expected Return | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Same as firm | 10% | Accept (10% > 8.3%) |
| B | Same as firm | 6% | Reject (6% < 8.3%) |

Both projects have same risk as firm, so WACC is appropriate hurdle rate.
