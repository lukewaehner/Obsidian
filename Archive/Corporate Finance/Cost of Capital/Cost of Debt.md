
---

## Cost of Debt

Expected return that new lenders would demand.

---

## Calculation

Use **YTM on existing debt** (market-based, forward-looking).

Do NOT use coupon rate (historical).

---

## After-Tax Cost

Interest is tax-deductible, so the effective cost is:

$$
r_D(1 - T_C)
$$

Where:
- $r_D$ = YTM
- $T_C$ = Corporate tax rate

---

## Example

BP bond: 15 years to maturity, $1,000 par, 6.2% coupon (semi-annual), price 90.07% of par.

Calculator:
- N = 30
- PV = -900.70
- PMT = 31
- FV = 1000
- CPT → I/Y = 3.65%

YTM = 3.65% × 2 = **7.3%**
