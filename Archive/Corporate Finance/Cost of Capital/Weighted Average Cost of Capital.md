## Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)

The firm's overall cost of capital.

$$
WACC = w_D \times r_D(1-t) + w_P \times r_P + w_E \times r_E
$$

Where:
- $w_D, w_P, w_E$ = Market value weights (must sum to 100%)
- $r_D(1-t)$ = After-tax cost of debt
- $r_P$ = Cost of preferred stock
- $r_E$ = Cost of equity

---

## Two Drivers

1. **Risk of underlying business**
2. **Leverage** (debt creates tax shield)

---

## Example

Epson:
- YTM on bonds: 4%
- Beta: 0.7
- Risk-free rate: 1%
- Market risk premium: 6%
- Target D/E: 0.3
- Tax rate: 34%

**Cost of Debt:** 4%

**Cost of Equity:** 1% + 0.7 × 6% = 5.2%

**Weights:**

$w_D = \frac{0.3}{1.3} = 23.08\%$

$w_E = \frac{1.0}{1.3} = 76.92\%$

**WACC:**

$WACC = 0.7692 \times 5.2\% + 0.2308 \times 4\% \times (1-0.34) = 4.61\%$

---

## Assumptions

- **Average risk**: Project risk equals firm average risk
- **Constant debt-equity ratio**: Firm maintains target capital structure
- **Limited leverage effects**: Main effect is interest tax deduction
