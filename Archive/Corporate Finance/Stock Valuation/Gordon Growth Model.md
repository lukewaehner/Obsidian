## Gordon Growth Model

Simplest DDM: assumes dividends grow at a constant rate g forever.

---

## Formula

$$
P_0 = \frac{Div_1}{r - g} = \frac{Div_0(1+g)}{r - g}
$$

Where:
- $Div_1$ = Expected dividend next year
- r = Required return
- g = Constant growth rate

---

## Requirements

- r > g (otherwise price is infinite or negative)
- Growth rate must be sustainable forever

---

## Example

IBM: Annual dividend $3.90, expected growth 1%, required return 12%

$$
P_0 = \frac{\$3.90 \times 1.01}{0.12 - 0.01} = \$35.81
$$

Price in 5 years:

$$
P_5 = P_0 \times (1+g)^5 = \$35.81 \times (1.01)^5 = \$37.64
$$

---

## Dividend Growth Rate

$$
g = \text{Return on New Investment} \times \text{Retention Rate}
$$

Retention Rate = 1 - Dividend Payout Rate
