## Dividend Discount Model (DDM)

Stock price equals the present value of all future dividends.

$$
P_0 = \frac{Div_1}{(1+r)^1} + \frac{Div_2}{(1+r)^2} + \frac{Div_3}{(1+r)^3} + ...
$$

---

## Limitations

- Future dividends are uncertain
- Some firms don't pay dividends
- Many firms use share repurchases instead
