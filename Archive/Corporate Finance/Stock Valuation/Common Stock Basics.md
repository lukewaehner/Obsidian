
---

## Common Stock Basics

Share of ownership in a corporation.

---

## Rights

- Vote on election of directors, mergers, major events
- Receive dividends (proportional to shares owned)
- Same dividends per share for all shareholders

---

## Two Sources of Cash Flows

1. **Cash dividends**
2. **Future sale** (capital gain)

---

## One-Period Valuation

$$
P_0 = \frac{Div_1 + P_1}{1 + r}
$$

Where:
- $P_0$ = Current stock price
- $Div_1$ = Expected dividend next period
- $P_1$ = Expected price next period
- r = Equity cost of capital (required return)

---

## Returns

$$
\text{Total Return} = \text{Dividend Yield} + \text{Capital Gains Yield}
$$

$$
= \frac{Div_1}{P_0} + \frac{P_1 - P_0}{P_0}
$$
