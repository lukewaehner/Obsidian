## Zero-Coupon Bonds

Bonds that pay only face value at maturity with no coupon payments.

Also called "pure discount bonds."

---

## Price Formula

$$
P = \frac{FV}{(1 + YTM)^n}
$$

---

## YTM Formula

$$
YTM = \left(\frac{FV}{P}\right)^{1/n} - 1
$$

---

## Key Feature

Zero-coupon bonds trade at a discount to face value to compensate investors for time value of money.

**Example**: Buy $1,000 face value bond for $700. The discount ($300) represents the interest earned.
