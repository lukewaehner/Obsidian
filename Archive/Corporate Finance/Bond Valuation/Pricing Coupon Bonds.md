## Pricing Coupon Bonds

The YTM is the discount rate that sets PV of promised payments equal to current price.

---

## Bond Price Formula

$$
P = C \times \frac{1}{y} \times \left(1 - \frac{1}{(1+y)^N}\right) + \frac{\text{Face Value}}{(1+y)^N}
$$

Where:
- P = Bond price
- C = Coupon payment per period
- y = YTM per period
- N = Number of periods

---

## Calculator Inputs

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| N | Years × Payment frequency |
| I/Y | YTM / Frequency |
| PV | ? (solving for price) |
| PMT | Par × Coupon Rate / Frequency |
| FV | Face Value |

---

## Example: Annual Coupon

12-year bond, $1,000 par, 5% coupon, 10% YTM

- N = 12
- I/Y = 10
- PMT = $1,000 × 5% = $50
- FV = $1,000
- PV → **$659.31**

---

## Example: Semi-Annual Coupon

21-year bond, $1,000 par, 5.4% coupon, 3.4% YTM

- N = 21 × 2 = 42
- I/Y = 3.4 / 2 = 1.7
- PMT = $1,000 × 5.4% / 2 = $27
- FV = $1,000
- PV → **$1,298.45**
