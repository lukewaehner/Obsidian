## Other Cash Flow Considerations

### Opportunity Costs

The value a resource could provide in its best alternative use.

If using existing equipment, include the forgone sale value.

---

### Project Externalities

Cash flows when a project affects other parts of the business (positively or negatively).

Example: New product cannibalizing existing product sales.

---

### Sunk Costs

**Ignore sunk costs.** Unrecoverable costs that should not affect decisions.

If the decision doesn't affect a cash flow, the cash flow shouldn't affect the decision.

---

### Salvage Value (Liquidation Value)

Equipment may be sold at end of project life.

**Capital Gain** = Sale Price - Book Value

**Book Value** = Purchase Price - Accumulated Depreciation

**After-tax Cash Flow from Sale:**

$$
\text{After-tax CF} = \text{Sale Price} - (\text{Sale Price} - \text{Book Value}) \times t
$$

---

### Example: Asset Sale

Machine: Cost $9.6M, depreciated straight-line over 20 years.

After 8 years: Sold for $5.4M. Tax rate 25%.

**Accumulated Depreciation** = $480,000 × 8 = $3,840,000

**Book Value** = $9,600,000 - $3,840,000 = $5,760,000

**After-tax CF** = $5,400,000 - ($5,400,000 - $5,760,000) × 0.25 = $5,490,000

(Tax benefit from capital loss)
