
---

## IRR vs NPV

IRR works for standalone projects with conventional cash flows (negatives precede positives).

---

## When IRR Fails

1. **Unconventional cash flows**: Positives precede negatives
2. **Multiple sign changes**: Can result in multiple IRRs or no IRR
3. **Mutually exclusive projects**: IRR may rank differently than NPV

---

## Key Rules

- For **mutually exclusive projects**, choose highest NPV (not highest IRR)
- NPV and IRR can rank projects differently due to:
  - Difference in scale (NPV changes with scale, IRR does not)
  - Differences in cash flow timing

---

## Crossover Rate

The discount rate at which two projects have the same NPV.

At rates below crossover: one project dominates.
At rates above crossover: the other dominates.

---

## Bottom Line

**When in doubt, use NPV.** It directly measures value added to the firm.
