
---

## Payback Period

Time until a project pays back its initial investment.

---

## Calculation

Start at time zero, add cash flows until cumulative total reaches zero.

---

## Decision Rule

- **Accept** if payback < cutoff period
- **Reject** if payback > cutoff period

Shorter payback is better.

---

## Limitations

- Ignores time value of money
- Ignores cash flows after payback period
- Arbitrary cutoff period
