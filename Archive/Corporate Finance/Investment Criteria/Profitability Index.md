## Profitability Index (PI)

Used when resources are limited to rank projects.

Measures "bang for your buck."

---

## Formula

$$
PI = \frac{\text{Present Value of Future Cash Flows}}{\text{Initial Investment}}
$$

---

## Interpretation

- PI > 1: Positive NPV investment
- PI < 1: Negative NPV investment
- PI = 1: Zero NPV investment

---

## Example

Project costs $200, PV of future cash flows = $220

$$
PI = \frac{220}{200} = 1.10
$$

For every $1 invested, you get $1.10 in present value.
