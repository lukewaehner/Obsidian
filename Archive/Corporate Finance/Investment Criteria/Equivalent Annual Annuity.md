## Equivalent Annual Annuity (EAA)

Used when comparing projects with different lifespans.

A project with more negative NPV might last longer, meaning less frequent replacement.

---

## Formula

$$
EAA = \frac{NPV}{\frac{1}{r} \times \left(1 - \frac{1}{(1+r)^N}\right)}
$$

---

## Decision Rule

Choose the project with the highest EAA.

EAA converts NPV to an equivalent annual benefit, making projects with different lives comparable.
