## Internal Rate of Return (IRR)

The discount rate that makes NPV equal to zero.

$$
NPV = 0 = \sum_{n=0}^{N} \frac{C_n}{(1+IRR)^n}
$$

---

## Decision Rule

- **Accept** if IRR > cost of capital
- **Reject** if IRR < cost of capital
- **Indifferent** if IRR = cost of capital

---

## Example

Project: Initial investment $41.34M, cash flows $16M/year for 4 years.

Using calculator:
- N = 4
- PV = -41.34
- PMT = 16
- FV = 0
- CPT → I/Y = **20.1%**

---

## Interpretation

IRR is the "breakeven" discount rate. If cost of capital is below IRR, project adds value.
