## Rule of 72

Quick estimate for time to double an investment.

$$
\text{Years to Double} \approx \frac{72}{\text{Interest Rate (\%)}}
$$

---

## Accuracy

Rule is fairly accurate when:
- Interest rate is greater than 2%
- Years to double is greater than 2

---

## Examples

- At 6%: 72/6 = 12 years to double
- At 9%: 72/9 = 8 years to double
- At 12%: 72/12 = 6 years to double
