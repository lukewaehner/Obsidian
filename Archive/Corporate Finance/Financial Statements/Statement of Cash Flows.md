
---

## Statement of Cash Flows

Shows cash inflows and outflows over a period.

---

## Three Sections

### Cash Flows from Operating Activities

- (+) Net Income
- (+) Depreciation & Amortization
- (+) Increase in current liabilities
- (-) Increase in current assets

### Cash Flows from Investing Activities

- (+) Sales of long-term assets and investments
- (-) Purchases of long-term assets and investments

### Cash Flows from Financing Activities

- (+) Issuances of common and preferred stock
- (+) Increase in bonds/notes payable
- (-) Payment of dividends/debt
- (-) Stock buybacks

---

## Reconciliation

$$
\Delta\text{Cash} = \text{CF from Operating} + \text{CF from Investing} + \text{CF from Financing}
$$

$$
\text{Ending Cash} = \text{Beginning Cash} + \Delta\text{Cash}
$$
