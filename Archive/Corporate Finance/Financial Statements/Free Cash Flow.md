## Free Cash Flow (FCF)

The finance view of cash flow. Amount of cash that could be withdrawn without harming the firm's ability to operate.

$$
\text{FCF} = \text{Operating Cash Flow} - \text{Capital Spending} - \Delta\text{NWC}
$$

---

## Three Components

### 1. Operating Cash Flow

$$
\text{OCF} = \text{EBIT} + \text{Depreciation \& Amortization} - \text{Taxes}
$$

### 2. Capital Spending

$$
\text{CapEx} = \text{Ending Net Fixed Assets} - \text{Beginning Net Fixed Assets} + \text{Depreciation}
$$

### 3. Change in Net Working Capital

$$
\Delta\text{NWC} = \text{Ending NWC} - \text{Beginning NWC}
$$

---

## Interpretation

$$
\text{FCF} = \text{Cash Flow to Creditors} + \text{Cash Flow to Shareholders}
$$

Free cash flow is the cash available for all claim holders before making payments to debt holders and shareholders.

---

## Example

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Sales | $38,072 |
| COGS | $27,168 |
| Depreciation | $6,759 |
| Interest Expense | $3,050 |
| Tax Rate | 22% |
| Net Fixed Assets (Beg) | $22,790 |
| Net Fixed Assets (End) | $28,053 |
| Current Assets (Beg) | $8,025 |
| Current Assets (End) | $9,904 |
| Current Liabilities (Beg) | $4,511 |
| Current Liabilities (End) | $5,261 |

**EBIT** = $38,072 - $27,168 - $6,759 = $4,145

**EBT** = $4,145 - $3,050 = $1,095

**Taxes** = $1,095 × 0.22 = $240.90

**OCF** = $4,145 + $6,759 - $240.90 = $10,663.10

**CapEx** = ($28,053 - $22,790) + $6,759 = $12,022

**ΔNWC** = ($9,904 - $5,261) - ($8,025 - $4,511) = $1,129

**FCF** = $10,663.10 - $12,022 - $1,129 = **-$2,487.90**
