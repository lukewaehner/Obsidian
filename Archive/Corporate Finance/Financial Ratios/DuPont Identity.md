## DuPont Identity

Breaks down ROE into three components.

$$
\text{ROE} = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Total Equity}}
$$

$$
= \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Total Assets}} \times \frac{\text{Total Assets}}{\text{Total Equity}}
$$

$$
= \text{ROA} \times \text{Equity Multiplier}
$$

$$
= \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Sales}} \times \frac{\text{Sales}}{\text{Total Assets}} \times \frac{\text{Total Assets}}{\text{Total Equity}}
$$

$$
= \text{Profit Margin} \times \text{Total Asset Turnover} \times \text{Equity Multiplier}
$$

---

## Three Drivers of ROE

1. **Operating efficiency** - Profit margin
2. **Asset use efficiency** - Total asset turnover
3. **Financial leverage** - Equity multiplier
