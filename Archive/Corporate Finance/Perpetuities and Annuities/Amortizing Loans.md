
---

## Amortizing Loans

Loans with fixed payments that include both interest and principal reduction.

---

## Characteristics

- Every payment reduces principal
- As principal decreases, interest portion decreases
- As interest portion decreases, principal portion increases
- Principal fully repaid with last payment
- Principal always equals PV of remaining payments

---

## Most Common

- Mortgages
- Car loans
- Student loans

Usually quoted as APR with monthly compounding.
