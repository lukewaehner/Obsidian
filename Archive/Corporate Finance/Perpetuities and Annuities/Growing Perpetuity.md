
---

## Growing Perpetuity

A stream of cash flows at regular intervals that grows at a constant rate g forever.

---

## Present Value

$$
PV_0 = \frac{C_1}{r - g}
$$

Where:
- $C_1$ = Cash flow at end of period 1
- r = Discount rate
- g = Growth rate

---

## Requirement

Must have r > g for the PV to be finite.
