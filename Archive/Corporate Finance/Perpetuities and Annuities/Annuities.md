## Annuities

A stream of a fixed number of equal cash flows at regular intervals.

By convention, cash flows arrive at the **end** of each period.

---

## Present Value of an Annuity

$$
PV_0 = C \times \frac{1 - \frac{1}{(1+r)^N}}{r}
$$

---

## Future Value of an Annuity

$$
FV_N = C \times \frac{(1+r)^N - 1}{r}
$$

---

## Example

Mortgage of $200,000, monthly payments of $1,957.77 for 15 years. What is monthly rate?

Using calculator:
- N = 180 (15 × 12)
- PV = 200,000
- PMT = -1,957.77
- FV = 0
- CPT → I/Y
