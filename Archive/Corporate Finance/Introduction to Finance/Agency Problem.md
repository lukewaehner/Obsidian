## Agency Problem

Conflict between managers (agents) and shareholders (principals).

### How to Control Agency Problems

1. **Managerial Compensation Plans**
   - Stock grants
   - Restricted stock
   - Stock options

2. **Direct Intervention by Shareholders**
   - Board of Directors oversight
   - Government monitors

3. **Threat of Hostile Takeover**
   - Poor management can lead to acquisition
