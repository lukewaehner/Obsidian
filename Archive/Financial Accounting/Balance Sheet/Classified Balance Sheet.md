## Classified Balance Sheet

Organizes assets and liabilities by liquidity and timing.

### Asset Categories

1. **Current Assets** - Can be converted to cash within one year
2. **Long-term Investments** - Cannot be converted to cash within one year
3. **Property, Plant & Equipment (PP&E)** - Assets with long useful lives used in operations
4. **Intangible Assets** - Assets without physical substance (Copyright, Brand name, Goodwill)

### Liability Categories

1. **Current Liabilities** - Can be settled within one year
2. **Long-term Liabilities** - Obligations expected to be paid after one year

---

## Format

```
Assets
  Current Assets (ordered most liquid to least liquid)
  Long-term Investments
  Property, Plant & Equipment
  Intangible Assets
Total Assets

Liabilities and Stockholders' Equity
  Current Liabilities
  Long-term Liabilities
  Stockholders' Equity
Total Liabilities and Stockholders' Equity
```

Both sides must equal.
