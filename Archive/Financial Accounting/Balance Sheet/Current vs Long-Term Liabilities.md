## Current vs Long-Term Liabilities

### Current Liabilities

Can be settled within one year.

- Accounts Payable
- Notes Payable (due within 1 year)
- Wages Payable
- Interest Payable
- Unearned Revenue

The relationship between current assets and current liabilities helps understand liquidity.

### Long-Term Liabilities

Obligations the company expects to pay after one year.

- Long-term Notes Payable
- Bonds Payable
- Mortgage Payable
