## Liabilities

Creditor claims on assets. Obligations the company owes.

### Examples

| Account | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Accounts Payable | Obligation to pay suppliers |
| Notes Payable | Obligation to pay banks |
| Wages Payable | Obligation to pay employees |
| Interest Payable | Obligation to pay interest |
| Unearned Revenue | Obligation to provide service (cash received in advance) |
