## Types of Inventory

### Merchandising Company

Items owned and ready for sale to customers.

### Manufacturing Company

Items in the process of manufacturing:

1. **Raw Materials** - Basic goods not yet placed into production
2. **Work in Process** - Partially completed goods in production
3. **Finished Goods** - Completed items ready for sale

$$
\text{Raw Materials} \rightarrow \text{Work in Process} \rightarrow \text{Finished Goods} \rightarrow \text{Sold}
$$

### Service Company

No real inventory.
