## Lower of Cost or Net Realizable Value (LCNRV)

Inventory value can fall due to technology or fashion changes.

### Rule

When value of inventory < cost, write it down to NRV.

When value of inventory ≥ cost, do nothing.

### Net Realizable Value

$$
\text{NRV} = \text{Normal Selling Price} - \text{Estimated Cost to Complete and Sell}
$$

### Conservatism

Value inventory at the lower of cost or NRV to avoid overstating assets.
