## Inventory Systems

### Periodic Inventory System

- Balances depend solely on physical count ("stock-taking")
- Purchases are recorded
- Cost of goods sold calculated at period end:

$$
\text{Beginning Inventory} + \text{Purchases} - \text{Ending Inventory} = \text{COGS}
$$

**Purposes of period-end stock-taking:**

1. Determine inventory on hand at balance sheet date
2. Determine cost of goods sold for the period

### Perpetual Inventory System

- Both purchases and sales are recorded continuously
- Balance available at any time:

$$
\text{Beginning} + \text{Purchases} - \text{Sold} = \text{Ending Inventory}
$$

- Physical count used to verify records

**Purposes of period-end stock-taking:**

1. Check accuracy of perpetual records
2. Determine inventory lost to waste, shoplifting, or theft
