## Payroll Liabilities

### Employee Taxes

Company withholds amounts from employee pre-tax wages to pay government.

**Example:**

Gross Pay: $10,000
Net Pay: $8,000

```
Dr. Wages Expense           10,000
    Cr. Tax Payable                  2,000
    Cr. Wages Payable                8,000
```

### Employer Payroll Taxes

Company owes additional taxes for employee benefits (Social Security, Medicare, etc.).

This is a separate expense beyond the employee's gross pay.
