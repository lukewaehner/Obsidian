
---

## Bonds Payable

Interest-bearing notes issued by corporations, universities, and governments.

---

## Key Terms

- **Face Value** (Par Value): Principal due at maturity, usually $1,000
- **Maturity Date**: Date final payment is due
- **Contractual Interest Rate** (Stated Rate): Annual interest rate on the bond

---

## Bond Participants

- **Issuer**: Company borrowing money
- **Bondholder**: Investor lending money

---

## Types of Bonds

- **Secured**: With collateral
- **Unsecured**: Without collateral
- **Convertible**: Can be converted to common stock
- **Callable**: Can be redeemed before maturity

---

## Bond Pricing

Quoted as percentage of face value.

- Price of 97 = 97% × $1,000 = $970
- Price of 116 = 116% × $1,000 = $1,160

---

## Issuing at Face Value

When contractual rate = market rate.

**Issue $100,000, 5-year, 10% bond at 100:**

```
Dr. Cash                   100,000
    Cr. Bonds Payable              100,000
```

**Record annual interest:**

```
Dr. Interest Expense        10,000
    Cr. Interest Payable           10,000
```

---

## Issuing at a Premium

When contractual rate > market rate. Selling price > face value.

**Issue $100,000 bond at 102:**

```
Dr. Cash                   102,000
    Cr. Bonds Payable              100,000
    Cr. Premium on Bonds Payable     2,000
```

Total Cost of Borrowing = Total Interest Payments - Premium

---

## Issuing at a Discount

When contractual rate < market rate. Selling price < face value.

**Issue $100,000 bond at 98:**

```
Dr. Cash                    98,000
Dr. Discount on Bonds Payable 2,000
    Cr. Bonds Payable              100,000
```

Total Cost of Borrowing = Total Interest Payments + Discount

---

## Redeeming Bonds

Compare carrying value to cash paid.

$$
\text{Carrying Value} = \text{Face Value} + \text{Premium} \text{ (or } - \text{Discount)}
$$

- Carrying Value > Redemption Price = **Gain**
- Carrying Value < Redemption Price = **Loss**

**Example: Redeem $130,000 bond at 102, carrying value $117,500:**

Cash paid = $130,000 × 102% = $132,600
Loss = $132,600 - $117,500 = $15,100

```
Dr. Bonds Payable          130,000
Dr. Loss on Bond Redemption 15,100
    Cr. Cash                       132,600
    Cr. Discount on Bonds Payable   12,500
```

---

## Advantages of Bonds Over Stock

- Financing cost is lower (interest is tax-deductible)
- Does not affect corporate control
- EPS may be higher (fewer shares outstanding)
