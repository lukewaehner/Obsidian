
---

## Closing Entries

Transfer Revenues, Expenses, and Dividends to Retained Earnings.

### Process

1. Close Revenue accounts to Retained Earnings
2. Close Expense accounts to Retained Earnings
3. Close Dividends to Retained Earnings

---

## Example

Close Service Revenue of $10,000:

```
Dr. Service Revenue      10,000
    Cr. Retained Earnings        10,000
```

Close Wages Expense of $4,000:

```
Dr. Retained Earnings     4,000
    Cr. Wages Expense             4,000
```

Close Dividends of $1,000:

```
Dr. Retained Earnings     1,000
    Cr. Dividends                 1,000
```
