## Ethics in Accounting

Standards of conduct by which one's actions are judged as right or wrong, honest or dishonest, fair or not fair.

Effective financial reporting depends on ethical behavior.

- Investors rely on financial statements to make decisions
- If investors question the ethics of management, trust is lost

## Ethics, Market Failure and Regulation

| Event | Response |
| --- | --- |
| 1929 Stock Market Crash | Securities Exchange Act of 1933, 1934 |
| 2001 Enron Fraud | Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002 (SOX) |
| 2008 Financial Crisis | Dodd-Frank Act of 2010 |
