## Accrual vs Cash Basis Accounting

### Cash-Basis Accounting

- Timing of recording depends on cash flows
- Revenue recognized when cash is received
- Expenses recognized when cash is paid

**Pros**: Simple

**Cons**: Easy to manipulate; does not reflect true revenue and expenses

### Accrual-Basis Accounting

- Records revenues when earned (even if cash not received)
- Records expenses when incurred (even if not yet paid)
- Required by GAAP

## Fiscal Year vs Calendar Year

- **Calendar Year**: 1/1 to 12/31
- **Fiscal Year**: Any 12-month period chosen by the company
