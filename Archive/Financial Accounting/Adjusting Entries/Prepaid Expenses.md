## Prepaid Expenses

Payment made before the expense is incurred. Recorded as an asset until consumed.

---

## Example

8/12/2022: Company pays $60,000 for insurance covering September, October, and November ($20,000/month).

**On 8/12/2022:**

```
Dr. Prepaid Insurance    60,000
    Cr. Cash                     60,000
```

**In September (when insurance takes effect):**

```
Dr. Insurance Expense    20,000
    Cr. Prepaid Insurance        20,000
```

---

## Another Example

Company purchased a 2-year insurance policy on June 1, 2022 for $24,000 cash.

**On 6/1/2022:**

```
Dr. Prepaid Insurance    24,000
    Cr. Cash                     24,000
```

**On 12/31/2022 (7 months used):**

$24,000 / 24 months = $1,000/month

$1,000 × 7 months = $7,000

```
Dr. Insurance Expense     7,000
    Cr. Prepaid Insurance         7,000
```
