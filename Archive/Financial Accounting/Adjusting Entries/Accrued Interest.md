## Accrued Interest

Interest expense that has been incurred but not yet paid.

---

## Calculation

$$
\text{Interest} = \text{Principal} \times \text{Rate} \times \frac{\text{Time}}{12}
$$

---

## Example

Company borrowed $10,000 by signing a 12%, 1-year note on September 1, 2022.

**On December 31, 2022:**

Interest from Sept to Dec = $10,000 × 12% × (4/12) = $400

```

    Cr. Interest Payable        400
```
