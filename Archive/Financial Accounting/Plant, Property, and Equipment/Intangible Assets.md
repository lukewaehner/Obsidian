## Intangible Assets

Assets without physical substance.

### Types

- Patents
- Copyrights
- Trademarks and Trade names
- Franchises and Licenses
- Goodwill

### Accounting

- Recorded at historical cost
- If limited useful life: Amortized over useful life
- If indefinite life: Not amortized

### Amortization (Straight-Line)

$$
\text{Annual Amortization} = \frac{\text{Cost}}{\text{Useful Life}}
$$

Useful Life = Minimum of (useful life, legal life)

---

## Example

Company purchased copyright for $102,000. Useful life 6 years, legal life 30 years.

Amortization = $102,000 / 6 = $17,000/year

