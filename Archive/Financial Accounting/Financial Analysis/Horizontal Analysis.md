## Horizontal Analysis

Also known as **trend analysis**.

Evaluates financial statement data over a period of time to determine increases or decreases.

---

## Formulas

$$
\text{Change (\$)} = \text{Current Year} - \text{Base Year}
$$

$$
\text{Change (\%)} = \frac{\text{Current Year} - \text{Base Year}}{\text{Base Year}}
$$

---

## Purpose

Detect changes in financial relationships and significant trends.
