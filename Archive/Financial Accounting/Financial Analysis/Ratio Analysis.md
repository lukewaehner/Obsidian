## Ratio Analysis

Analyzes relationships between different components of financial statements.

---

## Liquidity Ratios

Measure short-term ability to pay debts.

$$
\text{Current Ratio} = \frac{\text{Current Assets}}{\text{Current Liabilities}}
$$

$$
\text{Inventory Turnover} = \frac{\text{Cost of Goods Sold}}{\text{Average Inventory}}
$$

$$
\text{Days in Inventory} = \frac{365}{\text{Inventory Turnover}}
$$

$$
\text{A/R Turnover} = \frac{\text{Net Credit Sales}}{\text{Average Net A/R}}
$$

$$
\text{Average Collection Period} = \frac{365}{\text{A/R Turnover}}
$$

---

## Solvency Ratios

Measure ability to survive long-term.

$$
\text{Debt to Assets Ratio} = \frac{\text{Total Liabilities}}{\text{Total Assets}}
$$

$$
\text{Times Interest Earned} = \frac{\text{Net Income} + \text{Interest Expense} + \text{Tax Expense}}{\text{Interest Expense}}
$$

$$
\text{Free Cash Flow} = \text{Operating Cash Flow} - \text{Capital Expenditures} - \text{Dividends}
$$

---

## Profitability Ratios

Measure ability to earn profits.

$$
\text{Return on Assets} = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Average Total Assets}}
$$

$$
\text{Return on Equity} = \frac{\text{Net Income} - \text{Preferred Dividends}}{\text{Average Common Equity}}
$$

$$
\text{Profit Margin} = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Net Sales}}
$$

$$
\text{Gross Profit Rate} = \frac{\text{Gross Profit}}{\text{Net Sales}}
$$

$$
\text{Asset Turnover} = \frac{\text{Net Sales}}{\text{Average Total Assets}}
$$

$$
\text{EPS} = \frac{\text{Net Income} - \text{Preferred Dividends}}{\text{Weighted Average Common Shares}}
$$

$$
\text{P/E Ratio} = \frac{\text{Market Price per Share}}{\text{EPS}}
$$

$$
\text{Payout Ratio} = \frac{\text{Cash Dividends on Common}}{\text{Net Income}}
$$

---

## Comparison Bases

- **Intracompany**: Within company year to year
- **Intercompany**: With competitors
- **Industry averages**: Against industry benchmarks
