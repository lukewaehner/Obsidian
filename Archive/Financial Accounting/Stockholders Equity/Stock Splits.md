## Stock Splits

A 2:1 split means each share becomes 2 shares, and price/par value is cut in half.

### Effects

- Increases shares outstanding
- Decreases par value per share
- No change to total equity

### Journal Entry

No journal entry needed.

---

## Comparison

| | Stock Dividend | Stock Split |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shares Outstanding | Increase | Increase |
| Par Value | No change | Decrease |
| Total Equity | No change | No change |
| Retained Earnings | Decrease | No change |
| Journal Entry | Required | None |
