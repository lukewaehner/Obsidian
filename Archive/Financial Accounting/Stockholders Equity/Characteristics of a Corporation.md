## Characteristics of a Corporation

An entity created by law, separate and distinct from its owners.

### Key Characteristics

- **Separate legal existence** - Can own property, borrow money, be sued, pay taxes
- **Limited liability** - Stockholders' liability limited to investment
- **Transferable ownership** - Stock can be sold at shareholder's discretion
- **Easy to obtain capital** - By issuing stock or bonds
- **Subject to regulations** - SEC, state laws, stock exchanges
- **Double taxation** - Corporation pays tax on earnings, shareholders pay tax on dividends

### Corporate Structure

$$
\text{Stockholders} \rightarrow \text{Board of Directors} \rightarrow \text{CEO/President} \rightarrow \text{Management}
$$

### Types

**By Purpose:** For-profit, Not-for-profit

**By Ownership:** Public, Private

### Formation

- Find a state to incorporate
- Submit "charter" for state approval
- Charter describes: name, purpose, types and number of shares authorized

### Stockholder Rights

1. Vote in elections and corporate actions
2. Receive dividends
3. Maintain proportional ownership (preemptive right)
4. Share in assets upon liquidation (residual claim)
