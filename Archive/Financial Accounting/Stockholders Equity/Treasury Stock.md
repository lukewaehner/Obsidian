## Treasury Stock

Corporation's own stock that has been issued, fully paid for, and reacquired.

### Reasons to Repurchase

- Pay employee stock compensation
- Signal confidence and boost stock price
- Increase EPS

### Effects

- Reduces outstanding shares (not issued shares)
- Reduces equity (contra-equity account)

---

## Purchasing Treasury Stock

Reacquire 100,000 shares at $20/share:

```
Dr. Treasury Stock      2,000,000
    Cr. Cash                    2,000,000
```

---

## Selling Treasury Stock Above Cost

Sell 1,000 shares (cost $8) for $10/share:

```
Dr. Cash                               10,000
    Cr. Treasury Stock                          8,000
    Cr. Paid-in Capital from Treasury Stock     2,000
```

---

## Selling Treasury Stock Below Cost

Sell 500 shares (cost $8) for $7/share:

```
Dr. Cash                                3,500
Dr. Paid-in Capital from Treasury Stock   500
    Cr. Treasury Stock                          4,000
```
