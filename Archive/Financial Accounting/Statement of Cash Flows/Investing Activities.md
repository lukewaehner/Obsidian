
---

## Investing Activities

Activities related to acquiring and disposing of investments, PP&E, and lending money.

---

## Cash Inflows

- Sale of PP&E
- Sale of investments
- Collection of loans

## Cash Outflows

- Purchase of PP&E
- Purchase of investments
- Making loans

---

## Example

1. Purchased equipment for $1,300 cash
2. Sold land (cost $100) for $120

```
Cash flows from investing activities:
  Purchase of equipment              (1,300)
  Sale of land                          120
Net cash outflow from investing      (1,180)
```
