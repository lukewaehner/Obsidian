## Capital Market Line (CML)

- A set of portfolios optimally combining risk free and market portfolio
- Every point is an efficient portfolio with the highest expected return for a given level of risk
- Slope of the CML is the [[Sharpe Ratio]] of the market portfolio

### Formula

$$
\text{Slope} = \frac{E(r_p)-r_f}{\sigma_p}
$$

The expected return-risk trade-off of the complete portfolio:

$$
E(r_c) = r_f + \frac{E(r_p) - r_f}{\sigma_p} \cdot \sigma_c
$$

---

## Example

Given $r_f = 7\%$, $E(r_p) = 15\%$, $\sigma_p = 22\%$:

$$
E(r_c) = 7\% + \frac{8}{22} \cdot \sigma_c
$$
