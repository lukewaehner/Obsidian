## Correlation

The tendency of the returns on two assets to move together.

![[Images/Archive/Finance/Investments/Efficient Diversification/Correlation and Covariance/IMG-20251223193053140.png]]

Correlation coefficient measures correlation and ranges from -1 to 1.

Imperfect correlation is the key reason why diversification reduces portfolio risk as measured by the portfolio standard deviation.

---

## Covariance

$$
\text{Cov}(R_X, R_Y) = E\left[(R_X - E[R_X])(R_Y - E[R_Y])\right]
$$

---

## Correlation Coefficient

$$
\rho_{X,Y} = \frac{\text{Cov}(R_X, R_Y)}{\sigma_X \sigma_Y}
$$

---

## Variance of a Two-Asset Portfolio

Using correlation:

$$
\sigma_p^2 = w_1^2 \sigma_1^2 + w_2^2 \sigma_2^2 + 2 w_1 w_2 \sigma_1 \sigma_2 \rho_{1,2}
$$

Using covariance:

$$
\sigma_p^2 = w_1^2 \sigma_1^2 + w_2^2 \sigma_2^2 + 2 w_1 w_2 \text{Cov}(R_1, R_2)
$$
