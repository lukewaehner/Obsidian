## Overview
Investment companies that pool funds from multiple investors, with professional management making buy/sell decisions. They serve as financial intermediaries similar to commercial banks and life insurance companies.

**Key Learning Objectives:**
- Understanding categories of investment companies
- Calculating NAV
- How mutual funds operate and finding information
- Computing after-fee returns
