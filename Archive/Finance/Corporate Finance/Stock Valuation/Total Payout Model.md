## Total Payout Model

Uses total payouts (dividends + share repurchases) instead of dividends alone.

---

## Formula

$$
P_0 = \frac{\text{Total Payout per Share}_1}{r - g}
$$

Where:

$$
\text{Total Payout} = \text{Dividends} + \text{Share Repurchases}
$$

---

## Example

Lightpoint Inc.:
- Net income: $39M, growing 6% forever
- Pays out 50% as dividends
- Repurchases 15% of net income
- 35M shares outstanding
- Cost of equity: 12%

**Total payout:**

$\$39M \times (0.50 + 0.15) = \$25.35M$

**Per share:**

$\frac{\$25.35M}{35M} = \$0.7243$

**Stock price:**

$$
P_0 = \frac{\$0.7243}{0.12 - 0.06} = \$12.07
$$
