
---

## Liquidity Ratios

Measure short-term ability to pay debts.

---

## Current Ratio

$$
\text{Current Ratio} = \frac{\text{Current Assets}}{\text{Current Liabilities}}
$$

Measures ability to meet short-term obligations. Higher ratio (>1) is generally good.

---

## Quick Ratio (Acid Test)

$$
\text{Quick Ratio} = \frac{\text{Current Assets} - \text{Inventory}}{\text{Current Liabilities}} = \frac{\text{Cash} + \text{A/R}}{\text{Current Liabilities}}
$$

Stricter test excluding inventory (not easily convertible to cash).

---

## Cash Ratio

$$
\text{Cash Ratio} = \frac{\text{Cash}}{\text{Current Liabilities}}
$$

Most conservative ratio. Assesses ability to repay in a crisis.
