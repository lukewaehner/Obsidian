
---

## Market Value Ratios

Measure market perception of firm value.

---

## Earnings Per Share (EPS)

$$
\text{EPS} = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Shares Outstanding}}
$$

---

## Price-Earnings Ratio (P/E)

$$
\text{P/E} = \frac{\text{Price per Share}}{\text{EPS}}
$$

---

## Price-Sales Ratio

$$
\text{P/S} = \frac{\text{Price per Share}}{\text{Sales per Share}}
$$

---

## Market-to-Book Ratio

$$
\text{M/B} = \frac{\text{Market Value per Share}}{\text{Book Value per Share}}
$$

---

## Enterprise Value-EBITDA Ratio

$$
\text{EV/EBITDA} = \frac{\text{Enterprise Value}}{\text{EBITDA}}
$$

Where:

$$
\text{Enterprise Value} = \text{Market Value of Stock} + \text{Book Value of Liabilities} - \text{Cash}
$$
