## Forms of Business Organization

### Sole Proprietorship

Owned by one person who is also the manager.

**Advantages:**
- Easy and inexpensive to form
- Subject to few government regulations
- Lower income taxes (taxed once at owner's rate)

**Disadvantages:**
- Unlimited personal liability
- Life limited to owner's life
- Hard to transfer ownership
- Difficult to raise capital

---

### Partnership

Owned by two or more people.

**General Partnership:**
- All partners have unlimited liability
- If one partner cannot pay, others are fully responsible

**Limited Partnership:**
- General partners run business with unlimited liability
- Limited partners are passive investors with limited liability

---

### Corporation

A separate legal entity created by a state.

**Advantages:**
- Unlimited life
- Limited liability (stockholders cannot lose more than invested)
- Liquid ownership (easy to transfer via stock)
- Easier to raise capital

**Disadvantages:**
- Double taxation (corporate tax + dividend tax)
- Higher costs to operate
- Separation of ownership and management
