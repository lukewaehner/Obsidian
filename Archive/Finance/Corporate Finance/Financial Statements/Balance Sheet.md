## Balance Sheet

Snapshots a firm's financial position at a point in time.

Summarizes what firm owns (assets), owes (liabilities), and the difference (equity).

$$
\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Equity}
$$

---

## Asset Classification

**Current Assets:** Life of less than one year (cash, inventory, A/R)

**Fixed Assets:** Long life (PP&E, buildings)

---

## Net Working Capital

$$
\text{NWC} = \text{Current Assets} - \text{Current Liabilities}
$$

- NWC positive: Cash available over next 12 months exceeds cash that must be paid
- NWC is usually positive in a healthy firm
