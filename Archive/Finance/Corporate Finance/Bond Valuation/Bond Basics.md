
---

## Bond Basics

A bond is a security issued in connection with a borrowing arrangement.

---

## Key Terms

- **Face Value (Par Value)**: Principal amount paid at maturity (usually $1,000)
- **Maturity Date**: When the bond expires and face value is paid
- **Coupon Rate**: Annual interest rate (stated rate)
- **Coupon Payment**: Periodic interest payment = Face Value × Coupon Rate
- **Yield to Maturity (YTM)**: Discount rate that equates PV of payments to current price

---

## Cash Flow Structure

**Year 0**: Pay bond price

**Years 1 to N-1**: Receive coupon payments

**Year N**: Receive final coupon + face value

---

## Coupon Frequency

- Annual coupon: One payment per year
- Semi-annual: Two payments per year (most common in US)
  - Each payment = (Face Value × Coupon Rate) / 2
