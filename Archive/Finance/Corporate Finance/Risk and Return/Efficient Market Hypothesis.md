
---

## Efficient Market Hypothesis (EMH)

An efficient market is one where security prices fully reflect all available information.

---

## Assumptions

- Many profit-maximizing participants analyzing securities independently
- New information arrives randomly
- Investors adjust prices rapidly to new information
- Expected returns include risk in the price

---

## Three Forms

### Weak Form

Prices reflect all **past** market information (price history, trading volume).

- Technical analysis cannot generate excess returns
- Past prices don't predict future prices

### Semi-Strong Form

Prices reflect all **public** information (financial statements, news).

- Fundamental analysis cannot generate excess returns
- Prices adjust immediately to public announcements

### Strong Form

Prices reflect **all** information (public and private).

- Even insider information is reflected in prices
- No one can consistently achieve abnormal returns

---

## Hierarchy

Strong form includes semi-strong which includes weak form.

Most evidence supports semi-strong efficiency for developed markets.
