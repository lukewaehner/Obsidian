## Beta and CAPM

### Beta (β)

Measures a stock's systematic risk.

$$
\beta_i = \frac{Cov(R_i, R_m)}{Var(R_m)}
$$

Beta = percentage change in stock return for each 1% change in market return.

- β = 1: Same risk as market
- β > 1: More volatile than market
- β < 1: Less volatile than market
- β = 0: No systematic risk (risk-free asset)

---

### Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM)

Relates systematic risk to expected return.

$$
E[R_i] = r_f + \beta_i (E[R_m] - r_f)
$$

Where:
- $r_f$ = Risk-free rate
- $E[R_m]$ = Expected market return
- $E[R_m] - r_f$ = Market risk premium

---

### Security Market Line (SML)

Plots expected return vs beta.

All fairly priced assets should lie on the SML.

- Assets above SML: Underpriced (buy)
- Assets below SML: Overpriced (sell)

---

### Example

Stock with β = 1.5, risk-free rate 2%, market return 8%

$$
E[R] = 2\% + 1.5 \times (8\% - 2\%) = 11\%
$$
