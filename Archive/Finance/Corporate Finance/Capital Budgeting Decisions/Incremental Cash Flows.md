## Incremental Cash Flows

Focus on how a project changes the firm's cash flows.

---

## Incremental Earnings

$$
\text{Incremental Earnings} = (\text{Revenue} - \text{Costs} - \text{Depreciation}) \times (1 - t)
$$

$$
= \text{EBIT} \times (1 - t)
$$

---

## Key Principle

**Ignore interest expense** when calculating incremental earnings.

Reason: Separate investment and financing decisions.

---

## Example: Replacement Project

If Project B replaces Project A:

- Year 0 cash flow: $-20,000 - (-15,000) = -5,000$
- Year 2 cash flow: $16,000 - 10,000 = 6,000$

Must account for cash flows you forgo from the replaced project.
