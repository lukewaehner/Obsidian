
---

## Perpetuities

A stream of equal cash flows at regular intervals that lasts forever.

By convention, the first cash flow arrives at the **end** of the first period.

---

## Present Value of a Perpetuity

$$
PV_0 = \frac{C}{r}
$$

Where:
- C = Cash flow per period
- r = Discount rate per period

---

## Why It's Finite

If r > 0, the PV is finite because the PV of payments far in the future is negligible.
