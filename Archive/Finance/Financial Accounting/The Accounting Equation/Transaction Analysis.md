## Transaction Analysis

Analyze each transaction's effect on the accounting equation.

---

## Examples

**1. Sold common stock for cash to start business**

- Assets: + Cash
- Equity: + Common Stock

**2. Paid monthly rent**

- Assets: - Cash
- Expenses: + Rent Expense (decreases Equity)

**3. Purchased equipment on account**

- Assets: + Equipment
- Liabilities: + Accounts Payable

**4. Billed customers for services performed**

- Assets: + Accounts Receivable
- Equity: + Revenues

**5. Paid dividends**

- Assets: - Cash
- Equity: + Dividends (decreases Equity)

**6. Received cash from customers billed previously**

- Assets: + Cash, - Accounts Receivable

**7. Incurred advertising expense on account**

- Liabilities: + Accounts Payable
- Expenses: + Advertising Expense (decreases Equity)

**8. Purchased additional equipment for cash**

- Assets: - Cash, + Equipment

**9. Received cash from customers when service was performed**

- Assets: + Cash
- Equity: + Revenue
