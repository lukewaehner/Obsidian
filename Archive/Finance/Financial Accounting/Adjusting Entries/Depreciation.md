## Depreciation

The process of allocating the cost of a plant asset over its useful life.

$$
\text{Annual Depreciation} = \frac{\text{Cost} - \text{Residual Value}}{\text{Useful Life}}
$$

---

## Journal Entry

```
Dr. Depreciation Expense
    Cr. Accumulated Depreciation
```

---

## Example

Equipment costs $60,000, useful life of 5 years (60 months).

Monthly depreciation = $60,000 / 60 = $1,000
