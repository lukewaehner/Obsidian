## Expense Recognition Principle

Recognize expenses in the period when the company makes efforts to generate revenue, regardless of when cash is paid.

### Matching Principle

Expenses are reported at the same time as the revenues they are related to.

---

## Example

AAA paid $79,200 for expenses in 2022. Of that, $33,000 was for expenses incurred in 2021. Additionally, $46,200 of expenses incurred in 2022 will be paid in 2023.

**Cash-basis expenses**: $79,200

**Accrual-basis expenses**: $79,200 - $33,000 + $46,200 = $92,400
