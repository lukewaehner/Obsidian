## Writing Off and Recovering Receivables

### Write-Off (Allowance Method)

When an account is determined uncollectible:

```
Dr. Allowance for Doubtful Accounts    XXX
    Cr. Accounts Receivable                XXX
```

### Recovery of Write-Off

**Step 1: Reverse the write-off**

```
Dr. Accounts Receivable                XXX
    Cr. Allowance for Doubtful Accounts    XXX
```

**Step 2: Record collection**

```
Dr. Cash                               XXX
    Cr. Accounts Receivable                XXX
```

---

## Example

May 11, 2022: B. Jared's account of $1,300 written off as uncollectible.

```
Dr. Allowance for Doubtful Accounts   1,300
    Cr. Accounts Receivable               1,300
```

June 12, 2022: Jared pays the amount previously written off.

```
Dr. Accounts Receivable               1,300
    Cr. Allowance for Doubtful Accounts   1,300

Dr. Cash                              1,300
    Cr. Accounts Receivable               1,300
```
