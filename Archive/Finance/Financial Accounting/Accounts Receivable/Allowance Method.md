## Allowance Method

Estimate uncollectible accounts before they become fact.

### Step 1: Determine Uncollectible Amount

Two approaches:

- Single percentage (e.g., 5% of A/R will be uncollectible)
- Aging of A/R: Schedule by length of time outstanding with different percentages

### Step 2: Record in Allowance for Doubtful Accounts (ADA)

- Contra-Asset Account
- Normal balance: Credit

### Step 3: Journal Entry

```
Dr. Bad Debt Expense          XXX
    Cr. Allowance for Doubtful Accounts    XXX
```

---

## Example

On December 31, 2021, Allowance for Doubtful Accounts has a debit balance of $1,200. Company estimates 10% of A/R ($100,000) will be uncollectible.

Required ending balance in ADA: $100,000 × 10% = $10,000

Adjustment needed: $10,000 - (-$1,200) = $11,200

```
Dr. Bad Debt Expense         11,200
    Cr. Allowance for Doubtful Accounts   11,200
```

---

## Net Realizable Value

$$
\text{Net Realizable Value} = \text{A/R} - \text{ADA}
$$
