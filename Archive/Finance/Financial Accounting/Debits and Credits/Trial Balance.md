## Trial Balance

A list of accounts and their balances at a given time (from the ledger).

### Purpose

- Prove the mathematical equality of debits and credits after posting
- Uncover errors in journalizing and posting

### Trial Balance vs Ledger

- Trial Balance: Only has ending balances
- Ledger: Has full transaction history

### Format

| Account | Debit | Credit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cash | XXX | |

Debits must equal Credits.
