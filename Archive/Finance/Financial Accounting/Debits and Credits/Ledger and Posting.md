## Ledger and Posting

### Ledger

The entire group of accounts maintained by a company.

- Provides the balance in each account
- Keeps track of changes in balances

### Posting

Transferring the journalized debits and credits to specific accounts in the ledger.

$$
\text{Journals} \rightarrow \text{Posting} \rightarrow \text{Ledger}
$$

### T-Accounts

Each account type has a T-account that accumulates all debits and credits.

```
        Account Name
    ___________________
    |  Debit  | Credit |
    |_________|________|
    |         |        |
    |_________|________|
```
