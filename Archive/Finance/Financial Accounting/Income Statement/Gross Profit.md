
---

## Gross Profit

$$
\text{Gross Profit} = \text{Net Sales} - \text{Cost of Goods Sold}
$$

Also called **gross margin**.

---

## Gross Profit Ratio

$$
\text{Gross Profit Ratio} = \frac{\text{Gross Profit}}{\text{Net Sales}}
$$

Measures how much of net sales is kept after cost of goods sold.

---

## Example

| Company | Sales | COGS | Gross Profit | Op. Expenses | Net Income |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X | $75,000 | $45,000 | $30,000 | $19,200 | $10,800 |
| Y | $108,000 | $70,000 | $38,000 | $1,300 | $36,700 |
| Z | $163,500 | $83,900 | $79,600 | $39,500 | $40,100 |

Gross Profit Percentage for Company Y: $38,000 / $108,000 = 35.2%
