## GAAP and Standard Setters

**GAAP** - Generally Accepted Accounting Principles

### Standard Setters

- **FASB** - US accounting standard-setting body
- **IASB** - International Accounting Standards Board
- **IFRS** - International Financial Reporting Standards

### Enforcement

- **SEC** oversees securities markets
- Companies' reports (Form 10-K) are filed with the SEC
- Ensures ethical financial reporting and disclosure
