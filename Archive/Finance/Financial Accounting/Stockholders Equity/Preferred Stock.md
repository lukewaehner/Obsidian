## Preferred Stock

A class of shares with priority over common stock.

### Features

- Priority in dividend distributions
- Priority in liquidation
- Generally no voting rights

### Issuing Preferred Stock

Issue 2,625 shares of $100 par preferred stock at $120/share:

```
Dr. Cash                                     315,000
    Cr. Preferred Stock                              262,500
    Cr. Paid-in Capital in Excess of Par - Preferred  52,500
```

---

## Cumulative vs Non-Cumulative

### Cumulative Preferred

Unpaid dividends from previous years (dividends in arrears) plus current year must be paid before common dividends.

### Non-Cumulative Preferred

Only current year dividends must be paid before common dividends. Past unpaid dividends are lost.

---

## Example

Stock outstanding:
- Common: $1 par, 100,000 shares
- Preferred A: 3%, $100 par, cumulative, 5,000 shares
- Preferred B: 6%, $50 par, noncumulative, 3,000 shares

Dividends not paid last year. Total dividends declared: $50,000

**Preferred A (Cumulative):** 3% × $500,000 × 2 years = $30,000

**Preferred B (Noncumulative):** 6% × $150,000 = $9,000

**Common:** $50,000 - $30,000 - $9,000 = $11,000
