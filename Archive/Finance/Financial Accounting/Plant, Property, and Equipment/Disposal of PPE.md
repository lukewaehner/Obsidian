
---

## Disposal of PP&E

$$
\text{Acquisition} \rightarrow \text{Depreciation} \rightarrow \text{Disposal}
$$

### Steps

1. Calculate Net Book Value: Cost - Accumulated Depreciation
2. Determine Gain or Loss: Selling Price - NBV
3. Record Journal Entry

---

## Example: Loss on Sale

Truck: Cost $180,000, Accumulated Depreciation $100,000, Sold for $60,000

**Step 1:** NBV = $180,000 - $100,000 = $80,000

**Step 2:** Gain/Loss = $60,000 - $80,000 = -$20,000 (Loss)

**Step 3:**

```
Dr. Cash                          60,000
Dr. Accumulated Depreciation     100,000
Dr. Loss on Sale                  20,000
    Cr. PP&E - Truck                     180,000
```

---

## Example: Gain on Sale

Same truck sold for $90,000

**Step 2:** Gain = $90,000 - $80,000 = $10,000

```
Dr. Cash                          90,000
Dr. Accumulated Depreciation     100,000
    Cr. PP&E - Truck                     180,000
    Cr. Gain on Sale                      10,000
```
