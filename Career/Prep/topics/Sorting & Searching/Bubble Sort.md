## Idea

Repeatedly swap adjacent out-of-order elements until a full pass makes no
swaps.

## Implementation

## Complexity

O(n²) average and worst case.

## When to use it

Essentially never — it's dominated by insertion sort on every axis except at
very small n (roughly n ≤ 16), where the difference doesn't matter.

## Gotchas

Don't reach for it in an interview — naming it as "the terrible one" and
moving on is the expected answer.
