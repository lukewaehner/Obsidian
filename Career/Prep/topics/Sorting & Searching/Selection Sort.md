## Idea

Repeatedly find the minimum of the unsorted remainder and swap it into
place at the front.

## How it works

## Implementation

## Complexity

O(n²) average and worst case, even on already-sorted input — it always scans
the whole remainder.

## When to use it

Rarely, in practice — it makes the fewest swaps of any O(n²) sort, which
matters only when writes are far more expensive than comparisons.
