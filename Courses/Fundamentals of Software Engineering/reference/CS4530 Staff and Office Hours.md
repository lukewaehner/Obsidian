## Office Hour Logistics

- All TA office hours are **virtual**, hosted on Microsoft Teams via the [Khoury Office Hours app](https://officehours.khoury.northeastern.edu/). In-person TA hours are being explored, subject to student/TA interest and room availability.
- **VPN is required** to access the office hours app when off campus.
- Instructor office hours may be in person or remote.
- **No office hours during school holidays** — Spring Break, MLK Day, President's Day, Indigenous Peoples' Day, Veteran's Day, Thanksgiving Break.
- The live schedule is an embedded Google Calendar on the staff page.

> [!tip] Before you ask for debugging help
> Staff will ask you the five questions in [[CS4530 Scientific Debugging]]. Have them answered.
