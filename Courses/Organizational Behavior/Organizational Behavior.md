# Organizational Behavior

**ORGB 3201 — Organizational Behavior** · Fall 2026 · Northeastern (D'Amore-McKim)

**Course Focus**: Understanding and managing the human side of an enterprise. The course opens
by placing OB in the context of strategy and technology, moves to structuring the organization
and the teams within it, then narrows to the individual — leadership, decision-making,
negotiation, and the power and politics of the firm.
