## The Terms

Verbatim from the handout.

**Ball:** Language/sentences generally, without any addressivity.

**Shot:** Utterances - language with direction. Freadman says we don't exchange balls (plain
language) but shots (directed language). What kind of shot you choose to take, and your knowledge
of what shots work based on the rules, is your knowledge of uptake.

**Rules:** Freadman says the rules are akin to the genre conventions that make playing "the game"
of writing possible. For instance, the rules (the conventions of a genre) determine whether the
shot "counts" or is effective.

**Ceremonials:** The chronotope and kairotic moment of the "game" of writing. That is, the things
that make the writing take on certain kinds of meaning. She, for instance, notes that a game of
pick-up tennis and a game at Wimbledon are both tennis but have very different meanings and
stakes. In the same way, genres can be the same for two different kinds of writing, but have very
different meanings and stakes based on when and where they are written.
