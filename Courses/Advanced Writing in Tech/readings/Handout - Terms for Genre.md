## The Terms

Verbatim from the handout.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Genre** | Typified social action |
| **Exigence** | The social motivation for writing |
| **Addressivity** | the way language is directed |
| **Utterance** | language with direction - the building block of genres. |
| **Chronotope and kairos** | the social and cultural moment that forms writing, and the social and cultural moment that makes writing most effective. |
| **Uptake** | the ways we respond to a piece of writing |
| **Discourse community** | groups that share genres, language, etc. |
| **Antecedent genre knowledge** | genre knowledge that gets used to help compose in new and unknown genres |
| **Metagenre** | the larger structures of knowing and understanding genres OR a genre that explains how to write in another genre |

The lecture explains where each term comes from (Miller, Bakhtin, Bitzer, Freadman). See
[[Lecture - Intro to Genre]].
