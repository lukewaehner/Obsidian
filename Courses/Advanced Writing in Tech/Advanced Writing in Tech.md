# Advanced Writing in Tech

**ENGW 3302 — Advanced Writing in the Technical Professions** · Fall 2026 · Northeastern

**Course Focus**: Writing in professional, public, and academic genres for engineering and CS —
technical reports, progress reports, proposals, instructions, presentations, and technical
reviews. Run workshop-style, with audience analysis, critical research, peer review, and
revision as the core skills.

## Key Policies

### Submission
Email essays and reflections to the instructor **and** your small group by **midnight the day
they're due**. Peer review comments go directly to the students you reviewed. Canvas holds the
assignments for reminders only — **the email is the submission**.

### Memos
Every final draft ships with a 1–2 page writer's memo (2–4 pages for Assignment 4 and the
portfolio), due at the same time as the assignment it accompanies: what subject you chose and
why · the conventions of the genre · the choices you made as a writer · what you want the reader
to take away.

### Attendance
- Attendance is mandatory; class is discussion-based and revision-focused.
- **Two wellness days.** Every **2 absences beyond** those costs a classroom citizenship point, which can be made up with an extra act of citizenship.
- Excessive absences may result in a failing grade at the instructor's discretion, even with a legitimate excuse.
- Chronic lateness is treated as disrespect. Be on time.

### Late Work
- Miss your workshop without turning in the draft or talking to the instructor first → **you fail that workshop**.
- Extension requests go through the form on Canvas.
- **One week-long extension** allowed per semester.
- Late work affects your peer group, not just you.

### Behavior
Classroom runs on respect. Disrespectful behavior explicitly includes non-participation,
distracting the class, skipping class, late assignments, failing to return peer review, and
harmful or bigoted speech. Escalates to a required meeting, then to higher offices.

### Negotiation
All assignments can be negotiated. Unconventional approaches are allowed as long as you talk to
her first. Papers use the citation style of your choosing, Times New Roman 12pt, double spaced.

## About AWD

Advanced Writing in the Disciplines is built for students deep in their major and starting to
think about life after college — hence the 64-credit expectation and the advice to take it after
a first co-op. Student writing itself is the main object of study, and the course rejects
abstract or universal notions of "good writing" in favor of understanding how writing and
research actually function inside a specific discipline.
