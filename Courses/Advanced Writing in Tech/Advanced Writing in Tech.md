# Advanced Writing in Tech

**ENGW 3302 — Advanced Writing in the Technical Professions** · Fall 2026

**Course Focus**: Writing in professional, public, and academic genres for engineering and CS — technical reports, progress reports, proposals, instructions, presentations, and technical reviews. Run workshop-style, with audience analysis, critical research, peer review, and revision as the core skills.

## Assessment

**Specification grading** — a labor-based system. Details in the spec grade sheet.

Graded components beyond the projects:

- **Classroom citizenship** — earn **8 points** over the semester to show engagement with the material and the wider academic community
- **Engaged participation** — discussion, small groups, or written responses to readings on days you'd rather not speak; plus short assignments and respectful listening
- **Peer review workshops** — small groups, all semester; each group sets its own workflow. Some workshop days count as their own assignments and must be attended or made up.

## Key Policies

### Submission
Email essays and reflections to the instructor **and** your small group by **midnight the day they're due**. Peer review comments go directly to the students you reviewed. Canvas holds the assignments for reminders only — **the email is the submission**.

### Attendance
- Attendance is mandatory; class is discussion-based and revision-focused.
- **Two wellness days.** Every **2 absences beyond** those costs a classroom citizenship point, which can be made up with an extra act of citizenship.
- Excessive absences may result in a failing grade at the instructor's discretion, even with a legitimate excuse.
- Chronic lateness is treated as disrespect. Be on time.

### Late Work
- Miss your workshop without turning in the draft or talking to the instructor first → **you fail that workshop**.
- Extension requests go through the **form on Canvas**.
- **One week-long extension** allowed per semester.
- Late work affects your peer group, not just you.

### Behavior
Classroom runs on respect. Disrespectful behavior explicitly includes non-participation, distracting the class, skipping class, late assignments, failing to return peer review, and harmful or bigoted speech. Escalates to a required meeting, then to higher offices.

## About AWD

Advanced Writing in the Disciplines is built for students deep in their major and starting to think about life after college — hence the 64-credit expectation and the advice to take it after a first co-op. Student writing itself is the main object of study, and the course rejects abstract or universal notions of "good writing" in favor of understanding how writing and research actually function inside a specific discipline.
