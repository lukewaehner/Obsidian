## Spec

*Pass/fail requirements, straight from the syllabus.*

- [ ] 7 objects
- [ ] 150–200 word annotation per object
- [ ] 250–300 word Archivist's Note
- [ ] Each annotation and the Archivist's Note uses at least one term or theme from class, **bolded**
- [ ] Each annotation and the Archivist's Note explores the object's connection to the discourse community
- [ ] A cohesive overarching theme for the archive
- [ ] 1–2 page writer's memo, due at the same time as the assignment: subject and why it interests you · conventions of the genre · choices you made as a writer · what you want the reader to take away
