# Strategy and Action

**Course Focus**: How firms analyze their competitive environment, formulate a strategy, and take coherent action to build and sustain competitive advantage.

## Core Topics

### Foundations
- **[[Intro to Strategy]]**: Strategic positioning, trade-offs, and competitive advantage
- **[[What is Strategy? and Five Forces]]**: Defining strategy and analyzing industry structure *(WIP)*

## Key Concepts

### The Strategy Process
```
Analysis  →  Formulation  →  Implementation
(diagnose)   (guiding      (coherent
             policy)        actions)
```

### Competitive Advantage
```
Competitive Advantage      → Superior performance vs. competitors
Sustainable Competitive    → Advantage held over time (2y, 5y, 10y, >10y)
Advantage (SCA)              when value is inimitable or too costly to copy
```

### Two Levels of Strategy
```
Corporate Strategy → What businesses are attractive?
Business Strategy  → How should we effectively compete?
```
