- Execute immediately at best available price
- No price guarantee
- Bid price: price at which dealer will buy security
- Ask price: price at which dealer will sell security
---
Selling: I am **ask**ing for money, come buy from me
Bid: I will **bid** this amount for this item

---
For example, if you purchase $500 worth of Stock A, but the current cook has

Asks:
8 Shares @ 50
1 Share @ 100
1 Share @ 150

> You will get 9 shares, 8 @ 50, 1 @ 100, totaling $500 worth of stock.
