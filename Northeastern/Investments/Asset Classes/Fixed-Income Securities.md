Longer-term debt obligations of corporations and governments.
- Promise to make fixed payments on a pre-set schedule
	- Lives extend one year
	- Long-term treasury bond index bond
	- US government 20-year bond
	- Corporate bonds, auto asset-backed securities, student loans backed securities
Fixed coupon payments and final payment at maturity, except defaulting
Possibility of gain(loss) from fall(rise) in interest rates.
Depending on the debt issue, **illiquidity** can be a problem


> If you cannot sell securities quickly for their current market value, the market is said to be illiquid

**Exercise**:
100 bonds @ $1,000 FV that cost $900 each.
Coupon rate of 10% semiannually, maturing in 5 years

$$
\$ 1000 \times \frac{10\%}{2}
$$

At maturity you get, $1000 FV + $50 coupon, so $1050, and 100 times that

Timeline:
- @ Purchase (-900)
- 0.5 year: +50
- 1.0 year: + 50
- {..0.5}: + 50
- 5 year: + 1050
