## Notes:
- Time taken is $\leq c \cdot n^d$ for some fixed constant $d$
- Superset of everything we saw until now.
	0. Constant is $d= 0$
	1. Linear is $d=1$
	2. Quadratic is $d=2$
	3. Cubic will be $d=3$
	4. DroDroDur with 2 drones is $d=\frac{1}{2}$
- Considered practical and efficient in the Algorithms world
- Generally true, but any $d \gt 3$ or so, it starts to become bad in practice.
- Usually, when polynomial time algorithms are found $d$ is subsequently improved
- Notable: checking if a number is prime
- History of the AKS polynomial time primality checking algorithm
	1. 2002: $d$ is 12.
	2. 2004: brought down to $10.5$ and then down to $7.5$.
	3. 2005: brought down to $6$.
