## Notes:
- Time taken is $c \cdot n^2$ for some constant $c$
	- Note: $c \cdot n^2 + d \cdot n \leq (c+d)n^2$ is also quadratic time. **Worst case**.
	- Worse than linear, but mostly manageable. Better is often possible

- Examples: Naive sorts like insertion sort, selection sort, bubble sort
- Multiplying two $n$ digits integers (naively)
- We will use Divide and Conquer to get faster algorithms for above examples
Shows up when we **try all pairs**, when we use **nested loops** through the input, etc
