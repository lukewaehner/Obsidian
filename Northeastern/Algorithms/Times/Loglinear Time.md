## Notes:
- Time taken is $c \cdot n \log{n}$ for some constant $c$
- Better than quadratic but worse than linear
- Algorithms which have $n \log{n}$ runtimes are usually/often slightly more complicated

- Sorting algorithms like mergesort, heapsort, quicksort
- Common runtime when quadratic algorithms are involved via divide and conquer
